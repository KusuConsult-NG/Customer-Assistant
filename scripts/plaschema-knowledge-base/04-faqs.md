# PLASCHEMA — Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs)

---

## GENERAL QUESTIONS

**Q: What is PLASCHEMA?**
A: PLASCHEMA stands for the Plateau State Contributory Healthcare Management Agency. It is the government agency responsible for implementing the Contributory Healthcare Scheme in Plateau State, Nigeria, to provide affordable and accessible healthcare for all residents.

**Q: Who can enroll in PLASCHEMA?**
A: Any resident of Plateau State can enroll. There are plans for formal sector employees, informal sector workers (traders, artisans, farmers, etc.), vulnerable populations (BHCPF), and the poorest groups (Equity Program). Students in tertiary institutions can enroll via TISHIP.

**Q: Is PLASCHEMA only for Plateau State indigenes?**
A: No. Any resident living and working in Plateau State can enroll, regardless of their state of origin.

**Q: How do I know if my enrollment is active?**
A: Call the toll-free line **0700-700-1111**, visit the PLASCHEMA website at https://plaschema.pl.gov.ng, or check the PLASCHEMABeneficiary+ portal online. You can also visit any PLASCHEMA office.

## ENROLLMENT QUESTIONS

**Q: How do I enroll in PLASCHEMA?**
A:
1. Choose the plan that fits your situation (Formal, Informal, BHCPF, or Equity)
2. Fill out the enrollment form — online at https://enrollments.plaschema.app or at any PLASCHEMA office
3. Submit required documents: passport photograph, valid ID card, NIN (National Identification Number), birth certificates for children
4. Make payment where applicable (Informal Sector: ₦12,000/individual or ₦50,000/family annually)
5. Biometric capture (fingerprints and photograph) at an enrollment centre
6. Receive your PLASCHEMA ID card
7. Select your Primary Healthcare Facility (PHCP)

**Q: What documents do I need to enroll?**
A: Passport photograph, a valid government-issued ID card (NIN slip, voter's card, driver's license, or international passport), National Identification Number (NIN), and birth certificates for children under 18.

**Q: How long does enrollment take?**
A: The process typically takes 1–3 working days after submission of all required documents and payment (where applicable).

**Q: Can my family enroll together?**
A: Yes. The Informal Sector family plan covers the principal enrollee, one spouse, and up to four biological children under 18 years for ₦50,000 per year. Formal Sector plans also cover the principal, spouse, and up to four children.

**Q: What is the PLASCHEMA Pay platform?**
A: PLASCHEMA Pay is the official digital payment platform at https://enrollments.plaschema.app/pay/informal. It is used for paying informal sector premiums online securely.

## COVERAGE & BENEFITS QUESTIONS

**Q: What services does PLASCHEMA cover?**
A: PLASCHEMA covers:
- Outpatient consultations and treatment
- Inpatient (hospital admission) care
- Maternal health: antenatal care, delivery (normal and caesarean), postnatal care
- Child health: immunizations, growth monitoring, treatment for childhood illnesses
- Pharmaceutical (medicines) services
- Diagnostic services: laboratory tests, X-rays, ultrasounds
- Emergency care at any accredited facility
- Referral to secondary and tertiary hospitals

**Q: Does PLASCHEMA cover medications / drugs?**
A: Yes. Essential and prescribed medicines are covered. For Formal and Informal Sector enrollees, prescriptions are filled at the hospital pharmacy or any PLASCHEMA-accredited pharmacy at no cost. If a drug is not available at the facility, take your prescription and a copy of your PLASCHEMA ID to an accredited pharmacy. For BHCPF and Equity enrollees, only essential medicines on the approved formulary are covered. Where cost-sharing applies, enrollees pay only 10% of the drug cost.

**Q: Is emergency care covered?**
A: Yes. In an emergency, you can go to any PLASCHEMA-accredited facility — you do NOT need a referral letter for emergencies. Always present your PLASCHEMA ID card.

**Q: Does PLASCHEMA cover maternity / childbirth?**
A: Yes. Antenatal care, delivery (including caesarean section where medically required), and postnatal care are covered for all enrolled mothers.

**Q: Does PLASCHEMA cover surgery?**
A: Yes. Surgical procedures that are medically necessary are covered at PLASCHEMA-accredited secondary and tertiary hospitals.

**Q: What is NOT covered by PLASCHEMA?**
A: Generally excluded services include:
- Cosmetic or aesthetic procedures
- Experimental treatments not on the approved benefit package
- Specialized implants not on the formulary
- Treatment abroad
- Self-inflicted injuries
- Conditions arising from criminal activities
Always confirm your specific coverage by calling 0700-700-1111.

**Q: Can I go to any hospital, or must I use a specific one?**
A: You must access care through your registered **Primary Healthcare Facility (PHCP)** first. Your PHCP doctor will refer you to a secondary or tertiary hospital if your condition requires it. **Exception:** In an emergency, go directly to the nearest PLASCHEMA-accredited hospital — no referral is needed.

## PAYMENT & PREMIUM QUESTIONS

**Q: How much does PLASCHEMA cost for informal sector workers?**
A: ₦12,000 per individual per year or ₦50,000 for a family of 6 per year.

**Q: When do I pay my informal sector premium?**
A: Payment is annual (once a year). Renew before your expiry date to avoid a gap in coverage. Pay online at https://enrollments.plaschema.app/pay/informal.

**Q: How do formal sector employees pay?**
A: Contributions are deducted automatically from your salary — 1.75% from your pay and 3.25% from your employer, totalling 5% of your basic salary monthly.

**Q: What happens if I miss my informal sector renewal?**
A: Your coverage will lapse until you renew. You will not be able to access PLASCHEMA services at facilities until your premium is renewed and your enrollment reactivated. Call 0700-700-1111 for guidance.

## FACILITY & ACCESS QUESTIONS

**Q: How do I find an accredited hospital near me?**
A: Visit https://plaschema.pl.gov.ng/providers and filter by your LGA and category (hospital, pharmacy, laboratory). Or call 0700-700-1111 and ask a desk officer to find the nearest facility for you.

**Q: The hospital says it does not know about PLASCHEMA — what do I do?**
A: Ask to see the facility's enrollment agreement or call PLASCHEMA immediately at 0700-700-1111 to verify whether the facility is accredited and to report the issue.

**Q: What if the hospital is demanding money even though I have a PLASCHEMA card?**
A: This is a violation of your rights as an enrollee. Call **0700-700-1111 immediately** and report the facility name, location, date, and what happened. You can also email info@plaschema.pl.gov.ng. This will be escalated to the Director of Health Services, Standards and Quality Control.

**Q: What if the hospital says the drugs prescribed are out of stock?**
A: Ask the doctor to issue a prescription. Take that prescription and a photocopy of your PLASCHEMA ID card to any PLASCHEMA-accredited pharmacy near you to obtain your medications. Also report the stockout to PLASCHEMA at 0700-700-1111 so they can follow up with the facility.

**Q: I lost my PLASCHEMA ID card. What do I do?**
A: Call 0700-700-1111 or visit any PLASCHEMA office with your identification documents to request a replacement card.

**Q: Can I change my Primary Healthcare Facility?**
A: Yes, but changes are typically allowed at enrollment renewal or under special circumstances. Contact PLASCHEMA at 0700-700-1111 to initiate a change of PHCP.

## GRIEVANCE & COMPLAINT QUESTIONS

**Q: How do I file a complaint?**
A:
- **Call (24/7):** 0700-700-1111
- **Email:** info@plaschema.pl.gov.ng or support@plaschema.pl.gov.ng
- **Visit:** PLASCHEMA Headquarters, Jos, Plateau State
- **Online:** Via the PLASCHEMA website contact form

**Q: What information should I have ready when filing a complaint?**
A: Facility name and location, date and time of the incident, your PLASCHEMA ID or enrollment number, and a clear description of what happened (refusal of care, drug stockout, illegal payment demand, etc.).

**Q: How long does it take to resolve a complaint?**
A: PLASCHEMA aims to acknowledge all complaints within 24 hours and resolve them within 5–10 working days, depending on complexity. Urgent issues (like ongoing denial of emergency care) are prioritised immediately.

## ID CARD & VERIFICATION QUESTIONS

**Q: How do I verify my enrollment status?**
A: Call 0700-700-1111, check the PLASCHEMABeneficiary+ platform on the website, or visit any PLASCHEMA office.

**Q: My employer says I am enrolled but I have not received my ID card. What should I do?**
A: Contact your HR department first to confirm your enrollment form was submitted. Then call PLASCHEMA at 0700-700-1111 with your full name and employer details to track the status of your card.
