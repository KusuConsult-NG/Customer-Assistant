# PLASCHEMA Accredited Healthcare Facilities — Provider Network

## Overview

PLASCHEMA maintains a network of **400+ accredited healthcare providers** across all **17 Local Government Areas (LGAs)** of Plateau State. The network includes:

- **Primary Healthcare Centres (PHCs)** — First point of contact for all enrollees
- **Secondary Hospitals** — For referrals and moderate-complexity care
- **Tertiary Hospitals** — For specialist and complex cases
- **Accredited Pharmacies** — For prescription dispensing when hospital pharmacy is out of stock
- **Diagnostic/Laboratory Centres** — For blood tests, X-rays, ultrasounds, and other investigations

---

## How to Find Your Nearest Accredited Facility

1. Visit the **PLASCHEMA Provider Directory**: https://plaschema.pl.gov.ng/providers
2. Filter by **LGA** (e.g., Jos North, Pankshin, Shendam) and **Category** (Hospital, Pharmacy, Laboratory, etc.)
3. Or call **0700-700-1111** (24/7 toll-free) to ask a desk officer to locate a facility near you

## How to Access Care at an Accredited Facility

1. **Always carry your PLASCHEMA ID card** when visiting a facility
2. **Present your ID card** at the reception/registration desk
3. **Inform the facility** that you are a PLASCHEMA enrollee
4. **For Formal/Informal Sector enrollees:** Access through your registered **Primary Healthcare Facility (PHCP)** first; then get a referral to secondary/tertiary for complex cases
5. **For Emergency cases:** Go to any PLASCHEMA-accredited emergency facility — referral is NOT required in emergencies
6. **For drug prescriptions:** If your hospital pharmacy is out of stock, take your prescription and a photocopy of your PLASCHEMA ID to any accredited pharmacy

---

## Referral Process

- Enrollees must start with their **registered Primary Healthcare Facility (PHCP)**
- The PHCP doctor will issue a **referral letter** to a secondary hospital if needed
- Secondary hospitals refer to tertiary for specialist care
- **Exception:** Emergency conditions — report directly to any accredited hospital

## Provider Complaint / Performance Issues

If a facility refuses to treat you, demands illegal out-of-pocket payments, or does not have essential drugs:

1. Call **0700-700-1111** immediately (toll-free, 24/7)
2. Report: facility name, LGA, date, and nature of the problem
3. Email: **info@plaschema.pl.gov.ng** or **support@plaschema.pl.gov.ng**
4. Escalate to the **Director of Health Services, Standards and Quality Control** at PLASCHEMA headquarters if unresolved

---

## Becoming an Accredited Provider

Healthcare facilities wishing to join the PLASCHEMA network should:
1. Contact PLASCHEMA headquarters (0700-700-1111 or info@plaschema.pl.gov.ng)
2. Submit accreditation application with required documentation
3. Facility inspection and quality assessment will be conducted
4. Approval and listing in the provider directory upon successful evaluation
