# PLASCHEMA Health Plans — Complete Guide

## Overview

PLASCHEMA offers four distinct healthcare plans to cover all segments of the Plateau State population. Each plan is tailored to different employment or socio-economic circumstances.

---

## 1. Formal Sector Plan

**Who is it for?**
Employees in the formal sector — civil servants, state and local government workers, and employees of registered private organizations in Plateau State.

**Premium / Contribution:**
- Employee contributes **1.75% of basic salary**
- Employer contributes **3.25% of the employee's basic salary**
- Total combined contribution: **5% of basic salary** per month

**Key Benefits:**
- Access to 400+ accredited healthcare facilities across all 17 LGAs
- Comprehensive outpatient services (consultations, minor procedures)
- Full inpatient care coverage (admissions, surgeries)
- Maternal and child health services (antenatal, delivery, postnatal, immunization)
- Pharmaceutical services (essential and prescribed medicines)
- Comprehensive diagnostic services (laboratory tests, X-rays, scans)

**Family Coverage:**
Covers the principal enrollee, spouse, and up to four biological children under 18 years.

**How to Enroll:**
Contact your HR/payroll office or the PLASCHEMA headquarters directly.

---

## 2. Informal Sector Plan

**Who is it for?**
Traders, artisans, farmers, transport workers, market vendors, self-employed individuals, and any other worker in the informal economy.

**Premium / Contribution:**
- **₦12,000 per individual per year**
- **₦50,000 per family of 6 per year** (principal + spouse + up to 4 biological children under 18)

**Payment Method:**
Pay online via the **PLASCHEMA Pay** digital platform at **https://enrollments.plaschema.app/pay/informal**

**Key Benefits:**
- Access to 400+ accredited healthcare providers across Plateau State
- Comprehensive outpatient services
- Full inpatient care coverage
- Maternal and child health services (antenatal, delivery, postnatal)
- Pharmaceutical services (essential medicines covered)
- Diagnostic services (laboratory, radiology)
- Emergency care at any PLASCHEMA-accredited facility

**How to Enroll:**
1. Visit https://enrollments.plaschema.app/pay/informal
2. Fill out the enrollment form
3. Make payment (₦12,000 per person or ₦50,000 for a family)
4. Provide: passport photograph, valid ID card, NIN (National Identification Number), birth certificates for children
5. Biometric data (fingerprints and photograph) will be captured
6. Receive your PLASCHEMA ID card
7. Choose your Primary Healthcare Facility (PHCP) from the network
8. Start using services immediately after activation

---

## 3. BHCPF — Basic Healthcare Provision Fund

**Who is it for?**
Vulnerable populations identified through community-based targeting mechanisms, including:
- People who cannot afford regular premiums
- Very low-income households
- Communities identified through NHIA/government targeting surveys

**Cost:** **Free** — funded by the government

**Key Benefits:**
- Access to designated Primary Healthcare Centres (PHCs) across all 17 LGAs
- Basic outpatient services
- Essential medicines only (no out-of-pocket cost)
- Maternal and child health services (a priority focus)
- Treatment for common illnesses (malaria, typhoid, respiratory infections, etc.)
- Referral services for complex cases requiring secondary or tertiary care

**How to Access:**
Eligibility is determined through government targeting. Contact the nearest PLASCHEMA office or your LGA office to check eligibility.

---

## 4. Equity Program

**Who is it for?**
The poorest and most vulnerable groups in Plateau State, specifically:
- Pregnant women from the poorest households
- Children under 5 years of age
- Elderly persons above 65 years
- Persons with disabilities (PWDs)
- Other government-identified vulnerable groups (VGSHIP)

**Cost:** **Free** — fully funded by Plateau State Government and development partners

**Key Benefits:**
- Access to 400+ healthcare facilities across all 17 LGAs
- Basic and essential healthcare services
- Essential medicines at no cost
- Maternal and child health services (enhanced focus)
- Treatment for common illnesses
- Referral services for complex cases

**How to Access:**
Eligibility is determined by community-based targeting. Contact the PLASCHEMA headquarters at 0700-700-1111 or visit the nearest LGA office.

## How to Enroll (General Steps)

1. **Choose Your Plan** — Select the plan that fits your situation
2. **Complete Registration** — Fill the enrollment form with required documents (passport photo, valid ID, NIN, birth certificate for minors)
3. **Payment** — Pay your premium where applicable via the PLASCHEMA digital platform
4. **Biometric Capture** — Visit any PLASCHEMA enrollment centre for fingerprint and photo capture
5. **Receive ID Card** — Your PLASCHEMA ID card is issued after processing
6. **Access Care** — Present your ID card at any PLASCHEMA-accredited facility

---

## Drug / Pharmaceutical Policy

- For **Formal and Informal Sector** enrollees: Covered medicines are dispensed at no cost at the facility. If a hospital pharmacy is out of stock, you may access the prescription at any **PLASCHEMA-accredited pharmacy** by presenting your prescription sheet and a photocopy of your enrollment ID card.
- For **BHCPF and Equity** enrollees: Only essential medicines on the approved formulary are covered.
- **Cost-sharing for drugs (where applicable):** Enrollees pay only **10% of the cost**; PLASCHEMA covers the remaining **90%**.

## TISHIP — Tertiary Institutions Social Health Insurance Programme

**Who is it for?** Students in accredited tertiary institutions in Plateau State (universities, polytechnics, colleges of education).

**How it works:**
- Registration is handled through the institution's health centre
- Students pay an annual contribution (verify exact amount with your institution's health office)
- Coverage includes basic outpatient services, essential medicines, and referrals
- Students receive a health insurance ID card upon enrollment

Contact your school's health centre or call PLASCHEMA at **0700-700-1111** for more details.
