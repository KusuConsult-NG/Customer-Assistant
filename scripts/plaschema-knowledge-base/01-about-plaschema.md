# About PLASCHEMA — Plateau State Contributory Healthcare Management Agency

## What is PLASCHEMA?

PLASCHEMA stands for the **Plateau State Contributory Healthcare Management Agency**. It is the government agency responsible for implementing the Contributory Healthcare Scheme in Plateau State, Nigeria. PLASCHEMA's mission is to provide accessible and affordable healthcare to all citizens of Plateau State regardless of their income level or employment status.

## Mandate & Vision

- **Vision:** Universal health coverage for every resident of Plateau State.
- **Mission:** To provide affordable, accessible, and quality healthcare through an efficient contributory health insurance scheme.
- **Established under:** Plateau State Contributory Healthcare Management Agency Law.

## Provider Network

PLASCHEMA has over **400+ accredited healthcare facilities** across all **17 Local Government Areas (LGAs)** of Plateau State, including hospitals, clinics, pharmacies, laboratories, and diagnostic centres.

## Key Officials
