# PLASCHEMA — Grievance, Complaint & Enrollee Rights

## Your Rights as a PLASCHEMA Enrollee

As a registered PLASCHEMA enrollee, you have the following rights:

1. **Right to Access Care** — You must not be denied healthcare services at any PLASCHEMA-accredited facility when your enrollment is active and you present your ID card.
2. **Right to Essential Medicines** — You are entitled to prescribed medicines on the approved formulary at no charge (or at 10% cost for certain categories).
3. **Right to Emergency Care** — In any genuine emergency, you can access ANY PLASCHEMA-accredited hospital without a referral letter.
4. **Right to Referral** — If your condition cannot be managed at your Primary Healthcare Facility (PHCP), your doctor must issue you a referral to a secondary or tertiary hospital.
5. **Right to Quality Care** — You deserve respectful, dignified treatment from all PLASCHEMA-accredited healthcare workers.
6. **Right to Lodge a Complaint** — You have the right to report any violation, misconduct, or service failure without fear of victimization.

## Common Issues Enrollees Can Report

### 1. Extortion / Illegal Out-of-Pocket Demands
**What it is:** A healthcare facility demands cash payment for services or medicines that should be covered by your PLASCHEMA enrollment.

**What to do:**
- Refuse to pay if the service is covered under your plan
- Note the exact amount requested, the name of the staff member (if possible), and the date/time
- Call **0700-700-1111** immediately
- Email details to **info@plaschema.pl.gov.ng**

### 2. Refusal of Care
**What it is:** A PLASCHEMA-accredited facility refuses to treat you or register you as an enrollee.

**What to do:**
- Ask to see the facility's management or medical director
- Call **0700-700-1111** immediately while at the facility
- Document the name of the staff who refused and the reason given
- If it is an emergency, ask to be treated while the issue is resolved, or go to the nearest alternative accredited facility

### 3. Drug / Medicine Stockouts
**What it is:** The hospital pharmacy does not have the prescribed medicines in stock.

**What to do:**
- Ask the doctor for a written prescription
- Take your prescription + a photocopy of your PLASCHEMA ID card to any PLASCHEMA-accredited pharmacy nearby
- Also call **0700-700-1111** to report the stockout so PLASCHEMA can follow up with the facility

### 4. Poor Quality of Care / Negligence
**What it is:** Substandard treatment, disrespectful conduct, or medical negligence at an accredited facility.

**What to do:**
- Report to the facility's management first
- If unresolved, call **0700-700-1111**
- File a formal written complaint to **info@plaschema.pl.gov.ng**

## How to File a Formal Complaint

### Step 1 — Call Immediately
Call the 24/7 toll-free line: **0700-700-1111**

The desk officer will:
- Record your complaint
- Provide you with a complaint reference number
- Escalate urgent cases immediately

### Step 2 — Email Your Complaint
Send an email to **info@plaschema.pl.gov.ng** or **support@plaschema.pl.gov.ng** with the following details:

- Your full name
- Your PLASCHEMA ID number / enrollment number
- Your phone number
- Name and location of the facility
- Date and time of the incident
- Clear description of what happened
- Any evidence (photos, receipts, prescription documents)

### Step 3 — Escalation Path
If the complaint is not resolved within 5–10 working days:
1. Request escalation to the **Director of Operations** at PLASCHEMA headquarters
2. Further escalation: **Director of Health Services, Standards and Quality Control**
3. Visit PLASCHEMA headquarters in person: Jos, Plateau State

## Enrollee Obligations

While PLASCHEMA protects your rights, enrollees also have responsibilities:
- Always carry your PLASCHEMA ID card when visiting a facility
- Register with and access care through your designated Primary Healthcare Facility (PHCP) first (except in emergencies)
- Renew your premium (Informal Sector) before expiry to avoid gaps in coverage
- Report any abuse, fraud, or misconduct you witness at facilities
- Provide honest and accurate information during enrollment
