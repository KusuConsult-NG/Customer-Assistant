# MoE — Ministry of Education
Also known as: Ministry of Education (MoE), Ministry of Basic and Secondary Education, Ministry of Higher Education

## Current Leadership
- **Commissioner for Education**: Dr. (Mrs.) Kachollom Pyam Gang
- **Role Overview**: Originally appointed Commissioner for Higher Education in August 2023; assumed leadership of the unified Ministry of Education following the February 2025 merger of Secondary and Higher Education.

## Past Leadership & Administrative History
- **Former Commissioner (Secondary Education)**: Mrs. Grace Dongkum (August 2023 – February 2025 (reassigned to Finance))
- **Former Commissioner (Secondary Education)**: Mrs. Elizabeth Wapmuk (2019 – 2023 (Lalong administration))
- **Former Commissioner (Higher Education)**: Hon. Sylvester Wallangko (2019 – 2023 (Lalong administration))
- **Former Commissioner**: Mr. Athanasius Lozeng (2011 – 2015 (Jang administration))

Under Governor Simon Lalong and the early Mutfwang administration, the portfolio was split into Secondary Education and Higher Education. In the February 2025 cabinet restructuring, Governor Caleb Mutfwang merged both branches back into a single unified Ministry of Education.

## Mandate and Core Functions
Formulate, regulate, and supervise education policies across basic, secondary, technical, and tertiary institutions in Plateau State; enforce national standards; inspect schools; and promote educational excellence.

**Vision**: A world-class, inclusive, and technologically grounded educational system preparing all Plateau children and youth for 21st-century global opportunities.

**Mission**: To deliver accessible, equitable, and qualitative education through motivated teachers, modern learning infrastructure, and rigorous quality assurance.

## Public Services and Citizen Assistance
- Public secondary school placement, admissions, and inter-school transfer processing
- Accreditation, licensing, and annual regulatory inspection of private nursery, primary, and secondary schools
- Coordination of state basic education certificate examinations (BECE) and Mock SSCE
- Teacher posting, continuous professional development, and discipline in public schools
- Approval of WAEC, NECO, and NABTEB examination centres
