# PSPHCB — Plateau State Primary Health Care Board
Also known as: Plateau State Primary Health Care Board (PSPHCB), Plateau Primary Health Care Development Board

## Current Leadership
- **Executive Secretary**: Dr. Raymond Juryit
- **Role Overview**: Leading Primary Health Care Under One Roof (PHCUOR), routine immunization, and maternal healthcare.

## Past Leadership & Administrative History
- Predecessor leadership records maintained under historical state gazettes and executive transition archives.

Created to coordinate primary health centres across all 325 electoral wards under the Primary Health Care Under One Roof policy.

## Mandate and Core Functions
Manage, staff, and supervise all Primary Health Centres (PHCs) and basic health clinics; conduct routine childhood immunization campaigns; combat maternal and infant mortality; and deliver primary preventive healthcare.

**Vision**: A healthy grassroots population with access to functional, accessible, and well-equipped primary health centres in every ward.

**Mission**: To deliver high-impact, integrated primary healthcare services through community ownership and skilled primary healthcare workers.

## Key Departments and Directorates
- Department of Disease Control & Immunization
- Department of Maternal, Newborn & Child Health
- Department of Primary Healthcare Infrastructure & Logistics
- Department of Community Mobilization & Health Education

## Public Services and Citizen Assistance
- Routine childhood vaccinations (Polio, BCG, Pentavalent, Measles, Yellow Fever, HPV)
- Antenatal care, safe childbirth delivery, and postnatal tracking at ward PHCs
- Free distribution of insecticide-treated bed nets (ITNs) and malaria rapid diagnostic testing
- Community health outreaches and child nutrition supplementation
