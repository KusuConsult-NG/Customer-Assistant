# MHUD — Ministry of Housing and Urban Development
Also known as: Ministry of Housing

## Current Leadership
- **Commissioner**: Hon. Sylvanus Dongtoe
- **Role Overview**: Appointed in the cabinet restructuring to oversee public housing schemes and urban master planning.

## Past Leadership & Administrative History
- **Former Commissioner**: Hon. Joshua Laven Ubandoma (August 2023 – February 2025 (reassigned to Works))
- **Former Commissioner**: Alh. Dayyabu Garga (2019 – 2023 (Lalong administration))
- **Former Commissioner**: Mr. Samuel Galadima (2015 – 2019 (Lalong administration))

Responsible for public shelter delivery, civil service residential estates, and urban regeneration projects.

## Mandate and Core Functions
Formulate housing policies, develop affordable residential housing schemes, regulate urban development layouts, oversee architectural and structural engineering designs for state buildings, and conduct urban renewal.

**Vision**: A planned, orderly, and modern Plateau State with decent, affordable, and sustainable housing for all socioeconomic strata.

**Mission**: To eliminate housing deficits through public-private partnerships, smart building technologies, and orderly urban land development.

## Key Departments and Directorates
- Department of Architectural & Building Design
- Department of Public Housing Schemes & Estates Management
- Department of Urban Renewal & Slum Upgrading
- Department of Quantity Surveying & Building Economics
- Department of Planning, Research & Statistics

## Public Services and Citizen Assistance
- Applications and allocation of units in state civil servant and commercial housing estates
- Building plan reviews and structural safety compliance certification for commercial developments
- Urban regeneration and neighborhood infrastructure upgrading requests
- Public building valuation and tenancy management services
