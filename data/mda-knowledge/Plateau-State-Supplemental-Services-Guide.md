# Plateau State Government — Supplemental Services & Contact Directory

## 1. PSIRS (Plateau State Internal Revenue Service) — Full Details

**Full Name**: Plateau State Internal Revenue Service (PSIRS)  
**Executive Chairman**: Jim Pam Wayas  
**Headquarters**: Bank Road, Jos, Plateau State  
**Phone**: 08000000456 (State Citizen Line — press 2 for PSIRS)  
**Website**: psirs.pl.gov.ng  

### Tax Types & What You Pay
| Tax Type | Who Pays | Rate / Amount |
|---|---|---|
| Personal Income Tax (PAYE) | Formal employees (all residents earning in Plateau State) | Graduated: 7% on first ₦300k, 11% next ₦300k, 15% next ₦500k, 19% next ₦500k, 21% next ₦1.6M, 24% above ₦3.2M |
| Direct Assessment Tax | Self-employed, traders, artisans, entrepreneurs, professionals | Minimum ₦5,000/year; actual amount assessed by PSIRS desk officers |
| Development Levy | All taxable adults | ₦200 flat annual fee per individual |
| Land Use Charge | Property owners (developed or undeveloped land) | Rates set per LGA zone — apply at PSIRS headquarters |
| Capital Gains Tax | Sale of assets (land, buildings, shares) | 10% on net gains |
| Stamp Duties | Contracts, agreements, deeds, receipts above ₦10,000 | 1.5% on the instrument value |
| Motor Vehicle Licence | Vehicle owners | Per vehicle class: private car ₦3,000–₦7,500/year |
| Road Tax / Road Worthiness | Commercial and private vehicles | Per vehicle at PSIRS / MVAA office |
| Withholding Tax (WHT) | Businesses paying contractors, professionals | 5%–10% deducted at source, remitted to PSIRS |
| Business Premises Registration | Registered businesses | ₦10,000 (urban LGAs), ₦2,000 (rural LGAs) |

### Key PSIRS Services
- **e-TCC (Electronic Tax Clearance Certificate)**: Apply online at psirs.pl.gov.ng or any PSIRS office. Provide NIN, BVN, employment/income details. Issued within 3–5 working days.
- **TIN (Tax Identification Number)**: Free registration at any PSIRS office or via JTB portal (jtb.gov.ng). Required for contracts, banking, and importing goods.
- **PAYE Remittance**: Employers must remit employees' PAYE deductions on or before the 10th of each month.
- **Direct Assessment**: Self-employed persons file returns by March 31st each year.
- **Objections & Appeals**: Any taxpayer who disagrees with an assessment has 30 days to file a written objection at PSIRS headquarters.

## 2. Plateau State Scholarship Board — Full Details

**Mandate**: Award bursaries and scholarships to indigene students in accredited Nigerian tertiary institutions.  
**Office**: Ministry of Higher Education building, Jos.  
**Who Qualifies**: Must be a Plateau State indigene (father is from Plateau State), studying at an accredited institution.

### Scholarship Types & Amounts
| Award | Eligibility | Amount |
|---|---|---|
| State Bursary Award | ND/HND/Degree students at any accredited Nigerian institution | ₦30,000 – ₦50,000 per session (varies by year of release) |
| Federal Bilateral Scholarship | Nominated by state for overseas study (China, Russia, Ukraine, Morocco, etc.) | Full tuition + monthly stipend (varies by country) |
| Nigerian Law School Sponsorship | Plateau indigenes admitted to Nigerian Law School | Tuition coverage |
| Persons with Disability (PWD) Scholarship | Students with documented disabilities | Priority consideration for all awards |
| Postgraduate Award | Masters/PhD students at Nigerian universities | ₦50,000 – ₦100,000 per session (limited slots) |

### How to Apply
1. Confirm bursary application window is open (usually announced Feb–April each year).
2. Collect form at the Scholarship Board office in Jos OR download from plateau.gov.ng.
3. Submit: Completed form + admission letter + school ID + indigeneship certificate (from LGA) + birth certificate + BVN.
4. Screening interview (for some award categories).
5. Cheques or bank transfer disbursed to successful applicants' accounts.

## 7. Land & Property Services (PLAGIS / Ministry of Lands)

**Agency**: Plateau Geographic Information System (PLAGIS)  
**Parent Ministry**: Ministry of Lands, Survey and Town Planning  
**Commissioner**: Arc. Samuel Nanchang Jatau (Secretary to the Government also oversees)  

### Key Services & Fees
| Service | Processing Time | Approximate Fee |
|---|---|---|
| Certificate of Occupancy (C of O) application | 30–90 days | ₦50,000–₦500,000+ depending on plot size & zone |
| e-C of O (Electronic Certificate of Occupancy) | 14–30 days (fast track) | Additional ₦25,000 for digital issuance |
| Search Report / Land Title Verification | 5–10 working days | ₦15,000 |
| Deed of Assignment registration | 10–21 days | 3% of property value |
| Gazette of Layout / Survey Plan approval | Variable | Surveyor General sets fee |
| Right of Occupancy (R of O) renewal | Annual | Assessed by PLAGIS |

### How to Apply for C of O
1. Submit application at Ministry of Lands Survey office, No. 11 Joseph Gomwalk Road, Jos.
2. Attach: Survey plan, evidence of ownership, tax clearance certificate, development plan approval.
3. Pay assessment fee at designated bank (Remita or bank draft to Plateau State).
4. Site inspection by Ministry officials.
5. Executive Governor signs the C of O.
6. Collect C of O at the Ministry (or digital version via PLAGIS portal).

## 10. Frequently Asked Citizen Questions (FAQ)

**Q: How do I get a state indigeneship certificate?**  
A: Visit your LGA headquarters (e.g., Jos North LGA office at Murtala Mohammed Way, Jos) with your birth certificate or sworn affidavit, two passport photographs, and a letter of identification from your village head or ward head. Processing takes 1–5 working days. Fee is usually ₦1,000–₦3,000 at the LGA.

**Q: How do I register for PLASCHEMA as an informal sector worker?**  
A: Visit any PLASCHEMA registration desk (at JUTH, PSH, Plateau Specialist Hospital, or any PLASCHEMA partner bank) with your NIN slip, one passport photo, and ₦12,000 for individual registration (or ₦50,000 for family). You'll receive a PLASCHEMA card activated within 24 hours.

**Q: Where do I get my NIN (National Identity Number)?**  
A: Visit any NIMC enrolment centre. The main Jos centre is at the NIMC office on Yakubu Gowon Way, Jos. Bring your birth certificate or age declaration, one passport photo, and a valid ID (driver's licence, voter's card, or passport). No fee.

**Q: How do I report a road that needs repair or infrastructure complaint?**  
A: Call the Citizen Information Centre on 08000000456. The agent will log your complaint and forward it to the Ministry of Works or the relevant agency. Alternatively, visit the Ministry of Works at the Plateau State Secretariat.

**Q: How do I track the status of my job application to the civil service?**  
A: Contact the Plateau State Civil Service Commission at 07031100220 or visit their office at the Secretariat Complex, Jos. All vacancies are published in state newspapers and on plateau.gov.ng.

**Q: What is the number of local government areas in Plateau State?**  
A: Plateau State has exactly 17 Local Government Areas divided into three Senatorial Districts: Plateau North (Jos North, Jos South, Jos East, Bassa, Barkin Ladi, Riyom), Plateau Central (Mangu, Bokkos, Pankshin, Kanke, Kanam), and Plateau South (Shendam, Qua'an Pan, Langtang North, Langtang South, Mikang, Wase).

**Q: Who is the current Governor of Plateau State?**  
A: His Excellency, Barr. Caleb Manasseh Mutfwang, elected on May 29, 2023 under the Peoples Democratic Party (PDP). His Deputy is Her Excellency, Ngo Josephine Chundung Piyo.

**Q: Who is the Speaker of the Plateau State House of Assembly?**  
A: Rt. Hon. Na'anlong Daniel, representing Mikang State Constituency. He was elected Speaker on July 2, 2025, succeeding the former Speaker, Hon. Gabriel Dewan.

**Q: What is the DG of PLASCHEMA?**  
A: Dr. Dawal Solomon Kwande is the Director-General of PLASCHEMA, appointed by Governor Caleb Mutfwang.

**Q: How do I access government scholarships?**  
A: Contact the Plateau State Scholarship Board at the Ministry of Higher Education, Jos. Applications are typically open February–April each year. You must be a Plateau State indigene and enrolled in an accredited Nigerian institution. Bursary amounts range from ₦30,000 to ₦50,000 per session.
