# PSDMCMA — Plateau State Drugs and Medical Commodities Management Agency
Also known as: Plateau State Drugs and Medical Commodities Management Agency (PSDMCMA), Plateau State Drugs and Commodities Management Agency

## Current Leadership
- **Executive Secretary**: Pharm Kim Jerry Bot
- **Role Overview**: Leading state pooled drug procurement, drug revolving funds (DRF), and pharmaceutical quality control.

## Past Leadership & Administrative History
- **Former Director (Pharmaceutical Services)**: Pharm Gyang Pam (Pre-creation of Agency)

Established to solve drug stockouts and eradicate counterfeit medicines in public health facilities through a sustainable drug revolving fund.

## Mandate and Core Functions
Procure, warehouse, and distribute quality, safe, and cost-effective essential pharmaceuticals and surgical consumables to all primary, secondary, and tertiary public healthcare institutions in Plateau State.

**Vision**: An uninterrupted supply chain of certified, high-quality, and affordable medicines across every public healthcare facility in Plateau State.

**Mission**: To eliminate stockouts and fake medicines through transparent bulk procurement, modern cold-chain warehousing, and automated distribution logistics.

## Key Departments and Directorates
- Department of Pooled Procurement & Supply Chain Logistics
- Department of Quality Assurance & Pharmaceutical Testing
- Department of Warehousing & Inventory Control
- Department of Drug Revolving Fund (DRF) Accounts
- Department of Monitoring & Distribution

## Public Services and Citizen Assistance
- Ensuring availability of affordable, laboratory-tested authentic medicines in public General Hospitals and Primary Health Centres
- Central warehouse pooled supply of emergency epidemic drugs (antivenom, cholera kits, malaria treatments, maternal delivery packs)
- Investigative quality checks on suspected counterfeit drugs reported in public facilities

## Current Projects, Strategic Initiatives & Ongoing Activities
- Central Medical Commodities Supply Chain Modernization: Automated logistics and inventory tracking system connecting central warehouses to all state general hospitals and primary health facilities, preventing medicine stockouts.
- Anti-Counterfeit Drug Testing & Quality Assurance Laboratory: Rapid testing and forensic batch verification of pharmaceuticals distributed in Plateau State.
