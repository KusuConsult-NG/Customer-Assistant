# PSEC — Plateau State Energy Corporation
Also known as: Plateau State Energy Corporation (PSEC), Plateau Energy Corporation

## Current Leadership
- **Director General / Managing Director & CEO**: Engr. Ponzing Bindong Gamde
- **Role Overview**: Appointed February 2024 by Governor Mutfwang. Victor During Dagett serves as Board Chairman (inaugurated July 2025).

## Past Leadership & Administrative History
- **Former Coordinator**: Engr. Gyang Pam (Pre-2023)

Created to harness Plateau State's enormous hydro, solar, and wind energy potentials following the constitutional devolution of electricity to states under the Electricity Act 2023.

## Mandate and Core Functions
Develop state power policies, formulate the Plateau State Electricity Market regulations, develop off-grid and on-grid renewable energy projects, install solar street lighting, and attract private power developers.

**Vision**: A fully electrified Plateau State driven by clean, renewable, and decentralised energy powering rural and urban economic growth.

**Mission**: To exploit renewable energy potentials (hydro, solar, biomass), construct mini-grids, and establish a competitive state electricity market.

## Key Departments and Directorates
- Department of Renewable Energy & Solar Projects
- Department of Small Hydro & Power Generation
- Department of Electricity Market Regulations & IPP Liaison
- Department of Rural Electrification & Energy Planning

## Public Services and Citizen Assistance
- Rural community mini-grid electrification requests and solar power feasibility assessments
- Permits and licensing for independent power producers (IPPs) and solar mini-grid developers
- Maintenance of metropolitan solar streetlights along commercial traffic corridors
- Consultancy on industrial energy efficiency and captive power setups
