# PSWB — Plateau State Water Board
Also known as: Plateau State Water Board (PSWB), State Water Board

## Current Leadership
- **General Manager**: Engr. Eugene Pam
- **Role Overview**: Leading urban water treatment, pipe rehabilitation, and clean drinking water distribution.

## Past Leadership & Administrative History
- **Former General Manager**: Engr. David Wuyep (Pre-2023)

Founded to operate the urban municipal piped water networks, Yakubu Gowon Dam treatment facility at Lamingo, and regional water supply schemes.

## Mandate and Core Functions
Treat and distribute potable drinking water across Jos-Bukuru metropolis and regional urban centres; maintain dams, reservoirs, and pipe networks; bill water consumers; and test municipal water purity.

**Vision**: Uninterrupted supply of clean, safe, and affordable piped water to every urban home and business in Plateau State.

**Mission**: To produce and distribute water adhering strictly to WHO and Nigerian Drinking Water Quality Standards.

## Key Departments and Directorates
- Department of Water Treatment & Quality Control
- Department of Distribution, Pipe Networks & Maintenance
- Department of Commercial Operations, Metering & Billing
- Department of Mechanical, Electrical & Dam Operations

## Public Services and Citizen Assistance
- New public municipal water pipeline connections and water meter installations
- Reporting burst water pipes, leakages, and municipal water supply interruptions
- Payment of monthly residential and commercial water bills
- Laboratory bacteriological and physicochemical water testing for private boreholes

## Current Projects, Strategic Initiatives & Ongoing Activities
- Jos-Bukuru Water Supply Scheme Rehabilitation: Upgrading the Lamingo and Yakubu Gowon Dam water treatment plants, replacing damaged distribution mains, and deploying digital bulk water metering.
- Rural Water Sanitation and Hygiene (WASH) Boreholes: Drilling and rehabilitating solar-powered motorized boreholes across rural communities in Plateau State.
