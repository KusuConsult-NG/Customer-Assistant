# Governor Caleb Manasseh Mutfwang — Comprehensive Projects, Achievements & Scorecard (May 2023 – Present)

Also known as: Governor Mutfwang Projects, The Time is Now Administration Achievements, Plateau State Current Projects, What has Governor Caleb Mutfwang done, Plateau State Infrastructure and Projects Scorecard.

## Overview of "The Time is Now" Administration
Since assuming office on **May 29, 2023**, His Excellency **Barr. Caleb Manasseh Mutfwang** (Executive Governor of Plateau State) and Her Excellency **Ngo Josephine Chundung Piyo** (Deputy Governor) have executed a comprehensive transformation agenda anchored on **Peace and Security, Infrastructure & Mass Transit, Agricultural Revival, Healthcare & Social Welfare, Education, Digital Economy, Tourism/Hospitality, and Civil Service/Fiscal Reform**.

---

## 1. Mass Transit, Aviation, Rail & Transport Transformation
1. **Tin City Metro Urban Bus Rapid Transit (BRT)**:
   - Procurement and deployment of **15 brand-new German-engineered MAN/Neoplan luxury city buses** operating across **8 major corridors** in the Greater Jos–Bukuru Metropolis (Terminus, British America, Rayfield, Bukuru, Maraban Jama'a, Farin Gada, Unijos, Tudun Wada, Dadin Kowa, and Vom).
   - Subsidized flat fare of **₦200 per trip** using contactless **Tin City Metro NFC Smart Cards**, moving over 10,000+ commuters daily and cushioning fuel subsidy removal.
2. **Plateau Riders Interstate Fleet Modernization**:
   - Acquisition of new air-conditioned passenger buses, terminal upgrades in Jos and Abuja, and digitized ticketing under **Plateau Express Services Group Ltd (PESGL)**.
3. **Revival of Jos–Bukuru–Kuru Commuter Rail Service**:
   - Partnership with the **Nigerian Railway Corporation (NRC)** to rehabilitate tracks and resume commuter train operations linking Jos, Anglo-Jos, Bukuru, and Kuru for affordable passenger and cargo transport.
4. **Yakubu Gowon Airport (Heipang) Upgrade & Abuja–Jos Flights Restoration**:
   - Landmark partnership with **ValueJet Airlines** (and FAAN) restoring scheduled commercial flights on the **Abuja–Jos** route alongside Lagos–Jos flights, cutting travel time and attracting investors.
   - Ongoing upgrade of airport fire-fighting, runway lighting, and cold-chain agro-cargo terminal infrastructure.

## 2. Road Infrastructure, Bridges & Urban Renewal (Ministry of Works, PLARAMA & JMDB)
1. **Metropolitan Jos–Bukuru Road Dualization & Asphalt Overlay ("Operation Zero Potholes")**:
   - Rehabilitation, dualization, and asphalt resurfacing of **over 35+ strategic urban roads and bridges** across Jos North and Jos South, including:
     - **Tudun Wada – Kabong – Rukuba Road** link corridors
     - **Rayfield – Zarmaganda – Dadin Kowa** networks
     - **Hwolshe /iyon / Secretariat Junction – Tudun Wada** bypasses
     - **Bukuru Express service lanes, Gyel, Du, and Anglo-Jos** industrial roads
     - **Jenta Adamu – Jenta Mangoro – Apata – Katako – Laranto** township roads
     - **Rantya – State Lowcost – Domkat Bali Road** rehabilitation
     - **UTD – Hill Station – Terminus – Ahmadu Bello Way – Murtala Mohammed Way** urban renewal and solar street-lighting
2. **Inter-LGA Arterial Highways, Rural Roads & Bridges (Across All 3 Senatorial Zones)**:
   - Ongoing construction and rehabilitation of inter-LGA highways and bridges across **Riyom, Barkin Ladi, Bassa, Jos East, Mangu, Bokkos, Pankshin, Kanke, Kanam, Shendam, Qua'an Pan, Mikang, Langtang North, Langtang South, and Wase**.
   - **Rural Access and Agricultural Marketing Project (RAAMP)**: Construction of hundreds of kilometers of all-weather rural feeder roads and river crossings linking farming hubs to markets across all 17 LGAs.
   - Procurement of motor graders and earth-moving equipment for rural road opening across local governments.
3. **Executive Order 003 — Greater Jos Urban Sanity & Jos Main Market Renewal**:
   - Clearing of illegal roadside shanties, restoration of traffic flow and pedestrian walkways at **Terminus, Ahmadu Bello Way, Katako, and Bukuru**, solar street-lighting, and phased reconstruction of the **Jos Main Market** and neighborhood markets.
   - Launch of **JMDB E-Permit Portal** for fast-tracked digital building approvals and structural integrity audits.

## 3. Agriculture, Food Security & Resettlement Farming (MARD, ASTC & PADP)
1. **200+ Truckloads of Subsidized Fertilizer & Inputs**:
   - Direct ward-level distribution of over **200 truckloads (120,000+ bags)** of subsidized NPK and Urea fertilizers, improved maize/rice/potato seeds, and herbicides to smallholder farmers across all 17 LGAs.
2. **BARI & Conflict-Affected Farmlands Cultivation (6,000+ Hectares)**:
   - Cultivation of over **6,000+ hectares** of previously abandoned farmlands in **Barkin Ladi, Riyom, Bokkos, Mangu, and Bassa**, using newly procured heavy-duty tractors, free land clearing, seeds, and Agro-Ranger security cover so displaced families can farm and return home safely.
3. **ASTC Revival & Tractor Mechanization Fleet**:
   - Revitalization of the **Agricultural Services and Training Centre (ASTC)** in Vom and Kassa, procuring modern tractors, boom sprayers, planters, and harvesters.
4. **Irish Potato Value Chain, Tissue Culture & SAPZ**:
   - Tissue culture seed multiplication and modern cold-storage facilities in **Bokkos, Mangu, and Barkin Ladi** to defeat potato blight, plus partnership with the **African Development Bank (AfDB)** on the **Special Agro-Industrial Processing Zone (SAPZ)** and **Heipang Inland Dry Port**.
5. **Creation of the Ministry of Livestock Development, Veterinary Services & Fisheries (2025)**:
   - Dedicated ministry established to modernize ranching (Wase Grazing Reserve), dairy, poultry, Panyam Fish Farm, and mass animal vaccination with NVRI Vom.

## 4. Healthcare & Universal Health Insurance (MOH, PLASCHEMA, PSDMCMA, PSSH & PSPHCB)
1. **Commissioning of the Pharma-Grade Central Medical Commodities Warehouse**:
   - Construction and commissioning of a world-class, temperature-controlled **Pharma-Grade Central Medical Store** in Jos under **PSDMCMA**, along with an anti-counterfeit drug quality assurance lab, guaranteeing genuine, affordable medicines across all state hospitals.
2. **PLASCHEMA Universal Health Coverage Expansion**:
   - **100,000+ Vulnerable Citizens Enrolled Free**: Pregnant women, children under 5, elderly (65+), persons with disabilities, and IDPs enrolled at zero cost under the BHCPF/Equity fund.
   - **Retiree Free Healthcare Scheme**: Free medical coverage for state and LGA retirees.
   - **Informal Sector Plan**: Affordable coverage at **₦12,000/year per individual** or **₦50,000/year for a family of 6**.
3. **Plateau Specialist Hospital (PSSH) & Zonal General Hospitals Upgrade**:
   - Major renovation and re-equipping of **Plateau Specialist Hospital Jos** (ICU, maternity, CT scan, digital X-ray, dialysis, Electronic Medical Records, solar power) and zonal **General Hospitals in Barkin Ladi, Mangu, Pankshin, Shendam, Langtang, Wase, and Bukuru**.
4. **325 Ward Focal Primary Health Care (PHC) Centres Revitalization**:
   - Upgrading one focal PHC per electoral ward across all 17 LGAs with solar power, vaccine cold-chain refrigerators, boreholes, and delivery rooms under the World Bank IMPACT project.

## 5. Peace, Security & Humanitarian Resettlement (Operation Rainbow, PSPBA, SEMA)
1. **Recruitment & Kitting of 600+ Operation Rainbow Operatives**:
   - Training, kitting, and deployment of over **600 community security operatives** and **Agro-Rangers** across all 17 LGAs, backed by patrol vehicles, motorcycles, and a 24/7 toll-free Early Warning Control Room (**0800 0022 3344** / **09064000112**).
2. **Support to Operation Enduring Peace (OPEP) & Conventional Security Agencies**:
   - Provision of operational vehicles, logistics, and intelligence technology (drones/GIS) to the military, Police, DSS, and NSCDC.
3. **Safe Return & Resettlement of IDPs**:
   - Humanitarian relief, building materials, and security reinforcement for displaced communities in **Bokkos, Mangu, Barkin Ladi, Riyom, and Bassa**.

## 6. Digital Economy, ICT & E-Governance (PICTDA, MOSTI, MOIC)
1. **2,500km Statewide Broadband Fibre-Optic Backbone**:
   - Public-private partnership with **Solutron Eastern Fibre Limited** expanding fibre-optic broadband from 900 km to **2,500 km** across all 17 LGAs.
2. **Code Plateau 6.0, Code Plateau on Wheels & 3MTT**:
   - Free training of thousands of youths in software engineering, AI, data science, UI/UX, and cloud computing, plus **LGA CBT & Innovation Hubs** (including Qua'an Pan Hub and Murtala Mohammed House Tech Hub).
3. **Plateau State Government Citizen Contact Centre (Nanpan AI — 08000000456)**:
   - Launch of the 24/7 multilingual AI-powered citizen helpline and public inquiry desk under the Ministry of Information and Communication.
4. **PLAGIS Digital Land Registry**:
   - Full digitization of cadastral maps and accelerated electronic **Certificates of Occupancy (e-C of O)** with a 50% regularization waiver under the Ministry of Lands, Survey & Town Planning.

## 7. Education, Youth, Sports & Scholarships (MoE, PSUBEB, PSSB, MYSD)
1. **400+ UBEC/PSUBEB Classrooms & Smart Model Schools**:
   - Construction and rehabilitation of over 400 primary and junior secondary classrooms, solar ICT labs, perimeter fences, and distribution of thousands of desks and textbooks across all 17 LGAs.
   - Implementation of the World Bank **AGILE** project upgrading secondary schools and keeping girls in school.
2. **Tertiary Institutions & Student Scholarships**:
   - Infrastructure and accreditation upgrades at **PLASU Bokkos, PLAPOLY Barkin Ladi, College of Education Gindiri, College of Health Tech Zawan & Pankshin, and CAST Kurgwi**.
   - Upward review and digital disbursement of **student bursaries** and **Nigerian Law School grants** via the Plateau State Scholarship Board.
3. **Sports & Youth Empowerment**:
   - Phased completion works on the **Zaria Road New Jos International Stadium**, upgrade of **Rwang Pam Stadium**, sponsorship of **Plateau United FC** and **Mighty Jets FC**, and technical skills bootcamps for 5,000+ youths.

## 8. Civil Service Welfare, IGR Growth & Democratic Governance (MOF, OHCS, PSIRS, PLASIEC)
1. **Clearing of Inherited Salary/Pension Arrears & ₦70,000 Minimum Wage**:
   - Payment of 4–6 months of inherited civil service salary arrears, prompt payment of monthly salaries on or before the 25th, clearing of promotion backlogs, and full implementation of the **₦70,000 New National Minimum Wage**.
2. **Historic IGR Growth via Digital Taxation (PSIRS)**:
   - Deployment of the **Central Billing System (CBS)** and **e-TCC portal**, growing Internally Generated Revenue from ~₦15B to over **₦38.36B in 2024** and a projected **₦62.54B in 2025** without strangling small businesses.
3. **2024 & 2025 Transformational Budgets**:
   - **2024 Budget of New Beginnings**: ₦314.86 Billion.
   - **2025 Budget of Sustainability and Economic Transformation**: ₦468.49 Billion (60.9% Capital Expenditure: Works & Transport ₦92.45B, Education ₦68.92B, Health ₦44.65B, Agriculture ₦39.20B).
4. **Peaceful Local Government Elections (October 9, 2024)**:
   - Conduct of peaceful, credible elections across all **17 LGAs and 325 wards** through PLASIEC, restoring democratic grassroots governance.

## 9. Water, Environment, Energy & Hospitality (PSWB, PEPSA, MECCMD, PSEC, MTC&H, PIPC)
1. **Jos–Bukuru Water Supply & SURWASH**:
   - Overhaul of **Lamingo and Yakubu Gowon Dam Water Treatment Plants** and rural solar boreholes across the 17 LGAs.
2. **Keep Plateau Clean & ACReSAL Watershed Restoration**:
   - New PEPSA compactor trucks clearing metropolitan refuse heaps, plus World Bank **ACReSAL** gully-erosion control and tree-planting across degraded sites.
3. **Hill Station Hotel, Plateau Hotel & BARC Farms Revitalization**:
   - PPP concessions and investment drives through **PIPC** and the **Ministry of Tourism** to rebuild **Hill Station Hotel** and **Plateau Hotel** into 5-star hubs and revive **Jos Wildlife Park** and **Pandam Game Reserve**.
4. **Plateau State Electricity Market & Solar Mini-Grids**:
   - Implementation of the Plateau State Electricity Law under **PSEC** to deploy solar mini-grids and independent power projects (IPPs) to hospitals, schools, markets, and off-grid communities.
