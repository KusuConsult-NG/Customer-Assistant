# Governor Caleb Manasseh Mutfwang — Comprehensive Projects, Achievements & Scorecard (May 2023 – Present, 2026)

Also known as: Governor Mutfwang Projects, The Time is Now Administration Achievements, Plateau State Current Projects, What has Governor Caleb Mutfwang done, Plateau State Infrastructure and Projects Scorecard, Roads and Bridges under Mutfwang, Matthew Tegha Plateau Projects Tracker.

## Overview of "The Time is Now" Administration (2023 – 2026)
Since assuming office on **May 29, 2023**, His Excellency **Barr. Caleb Manasseh Mutfwang** (Executive Governor of Plateau State) and Her Excellency **Ngo Josephine Chundung Piyo** (Deputy Governor) have executed a sweeping infrastructure and governance transformation across all **17 Local Government Areas (LGAs)** and **3 Senatorial Zones**, completing ~80% of critical inherited/abandoned projects dating back to 2012–2014 alongside flagship new roads, bridges, mass transit, hospitals, schools, water plants, and agro-industrial infrastructure.

---

## 1. Road Infrastructure, Bridges, Flyovers & Urban Renewal (Ministry of Works, PLARAMA, JMDB & RAAMP)
1. **Major Inter-Senatorial & Inter-LGA Roads Flagged Off / Reconstructed (2025–2026)**:
   - **Bisichi – Fan – Mangu Halle Road (41 km)**: Major reconstruction and upgrade from an abandoned 2012 surface-dressed design to a full durable asphalt highway linking **Barkin Ladi and Mangu LGAs** across all three Plateau Senatorial Districts (flagged off October 2026).
   - **Jebbu Bassa – Binchin – Buyo Road (39.7 km, Bassa LGA)**: Flagged off in February 2026 to open up agrarian communities in Bassa and boost inter-state trade connectivity with Kaduna State.
   - **Sabon Layi – Corner Shagari Road (2.3 km, Jos North LGA)**: Flagged off in February 2026 as part of inclusive urban township renewal.
   - **Utonkon Bridge & Adjoining Roads (Namus Junction – Rayfield Road & Utonkon – Abattoir Corridor)**: Completed and commissioned on **Democracy Day (June 12, 2025)** to eliminate chronic urban traffic bottlenecks in Jos.
   - **Revival of 49 Legacy / Abandoned Roads Across 6 LGAs (2025)**: Approved and mobilized contractors back to site on **49 long-abandoned road projects across 6 LGAs** (originally awarded as far back as 2014).
   - **Mararaban Jama'a – Pankshin – Shendam – Ibi Federal-State Highway Synergy**: Joint collaboration with the Federal Ministry of Works advancing the trunk highway linking Plateau Central and Southern zones to Taraba State and the North-East.
2. **Greater Jos–Bukuru Metropolitan Roads ("Operation Zero Potholes" — 35+ Urban Corridors)**:
   - **Farin Gada – Gada Biyu – Polo Roundabout – Police Headquarters Road**
   - **Gold and Base Roundabout – Rayfield Road** and **Rayfield internal network roads**
   - **Bukuru Low-Cost – Sabon Barki Road** and **Bukuru Express service lanes, Gyel, Du & Anglo-Jos** industrial corridors
   - **Tudun Wada – Kabong – Rukuba Road** and **Hwolshe – Secretariat Junction – Tudun Wada** bypasses
   - **Jenta Adamu – Jenta Mangoro – Apata – Katako – Laranto** township roads
   - **Rantya – State Lowcost – Domkat Bali Road**
   - **UTD – Hill Station – Terminus – Ahmadu Bello Way – Murtala Mohammed Way** asphalt overlay and solar street-lighting
3. **RAAMP Rural Roads (335 km) & Agro-Logistics Centres (₦2 Billion Counterpart Fund)**:
   - Approved **₦2 Billion** in state counterpart funding (May 2025) for the World Bank-backed **Rural Access and Agricultural Marketing Project (RAAMP)**.
   - Construction and upgrading of **335 kilometers of all-weather rural feeder roads** and cross-drainages/culverts across the 17 LGAs.
   - Groundbreaking and construction of modern **Agro-Logistics Centres** in **Shendam, Mangu, and Bassa (Jengre)**.
4. **Executive Order 003 — Urban Sanity, JMDB E-Permits & Jos Main Market Renewal**:
   - Restoration of traffic flow, pedestrian walkways, and solar street-lighting at **Terminus, Ahmadu Bello Way, Katako, and Bukuru**, phased reconstruction of **Jos Main Market**, and launch of the **JMDB E-Permit Portal**.

## 2. Mass Transit, Commuter Rail & Aviation (Ministry of Transport & PESGL)
1. **Tin City Metro Bus Rapid Transit (Phase 1 & Metro Buses 2.0)**:
   - Procurement and deployment of **15 brand-new German-engineered MAN/Neoplan luxury city buses** (Phase 1) plus the **Metro Buses 2.0** expansion batch commissioned on **June 12, 2025**.
   - Operates across **8 major Jos–Bukuru corridors** (Terminus, British America, Rayfield, Bukuru, Maraban Jama'a, Farin Gada, Unijos, Tudun Wada, Dadin Kowa, Vom) at a subsidized **₦200 flat fare** using contactless **NFC Smart Cards**, serving 10,000+ commuters daily.
2. **Plateau Riders (PESGL) Interstate Fleet Upgrade**:
   - Deployment of new air-conditioned buses, refurbished 14-seater Toyota Hiace buses, and security-tracked Toyota Sienna vehicles on **Jos–Abuja, Kaduna, Kano, and southern routes**, terminal upgrades in Jos and Abuja, and free holiday transport interventions.
3. **Revival of Jos – Bukuru – Kuru Commuter Rail Service**:
   - Partnership with the **Nigerian Railway Corporation (NRC)** rehabilitating tracks and restoring commuter train rides linking **Jos, Anglo-Jos, Bukuru, and Kuru**.
4. **Yakubu Gowon Airport (Heipang) International Cargo Upgrade & ValueJet Flights**:
   - Partnership with **ValueJet Airlines** restoring scheduled commercial flights on the **Abuja–Jos** route alongside **Lagos–Jos** connectivity.
   - Signed **MoU with FAAN** to upgrade **Yakubu Gowon Airport, Heipang** into an **International Agro-Cargo Airport** for exporting Plateau vegetables, potatoes, and flowers.

## 3. Healthcare, Hospitals & Universal Health Insurance (MOH, PSSH, PSPHCB, PLASCHEMA & PSDMCMA)
1. **Plateau State Specialist Hospital (PSSH), Jos — New Commissioned Complexes (June 2025)**:
   - Official commissioning of a newly constructed **Modern Diagnostic Laboratory Complex**, a new **Paediatric Ward**, and a new **Administrative Office Complex** at Plateau Specialist Hospital, alongside ICU, CT scan, digital X-ray, dialysis, solar power, and Electronic Medical Records (EMR) upgrades.
2. **105+ Primary Healthcare Centres (PHCs) Revitalized & New Cottage Hospitals**:
   - Comprehensive renovation, solarization, and re-equipping of **over 105 Primary Healthcare Centres (PHCs)** across the 17 LGAs (towards the 325 ward focal PHC target under the World Bank IMPACT project).
   - Elevation and upgrade of key facilities in **Tudun Wada (Jos North)** and **Wase** into functional **Cottage Hospitals**, alongside upgrades to zonal General Hospitals in **Barkin Ladi, Mangu, Pankshin, Shendam, Langtang, and Bukuru**.
3. **Pharma-Grade Central Medical Commodities Warehouse (PSDMCMA)**:
   - Commissioned a world-class temperature-controlled **Pharma-Grade Central Medical Warehouse** and drug quality-control laboratory in Jos to eliminate counterfeit medicines.
4. **PLASCHEMA Universal Health Insurance Expansion**:
   - **100,000+ vulnerable citizens** (pregnant women, children under 5, elderly 65+, PWDs, IDPs) and **retirees** enrolled on free healthcare, plus the **₦12,000/year individual** and **₦50,000/year family of 6** informal sector plans.

## 4. Agriculture, Food Security & Resettlement Farming (MARD, ASTC, PADP & Livestock Ministry)
1. **200+ Truckloads of Subsidized Fertilizer & Farm Inputs**:
   - Distribution of over **200 truckloads (120,000+ bags)** of subsidized NPK/Urea fertilizers, improved seeds, and agro-chemicals across all 17 LGAs.
2. **6,000+ Hectares BARI Resettlement Farming & Wase Grazing Reserve Reclamation**:
   - Tractor clearing and cultivation of **6,000+ hectares** of conflict-affected farmlands in **Bokkos, Mangu, Barkin Ladi, Riyom, and Bassa** with Agro-Ranger security cover, plus reclamation of volatile agricultural zones in **Wase** for large-scale investment.
3. **ASTC Tractor Fleet Revival, Irish Potato Tissue Culture & AfDB SAPZ**:
   - Revitalization of **ASTC** with modern tractors/harvesters, tissue-culture potato seed multiplication and cold storage in **Bokkos, Mangu, and Barkin Ladi**, partnership with **AfDB** on the **Special Agro-Industrial Processing Zone (SAPZ)** and **Heipang Inland Dry Port**, and creation of the **Ministry of Livestock Development, Veterinary Services & Fisheries (2025)**.

## 5. Education, Tertiary Institutions, Youth & Sports (MoE, PSUBEB, PLAPOLY, PLASU, PSSB & MYSD)
1. **Plateau State Polytechnic (Jos Campus) Commissioned Projects (June 2025)**:
   - Commissioned a newly constructed **1-Storey Administrative Block** and the comprehensively refurbished **Joshua Dariye Hall** at Plateau State Polytechnic, Jos Campus.
2. **Creation of the Senior Secondary Education Board, 400+ PSUBEB Classrooms & AGILE**:
   - Established the **Senior Secondary Education Board** to standardize secondary education, constructed/renovated **400+ UBEC/PSUBEB classrooms** and smart model schools across the 17 LGAs, and rolled out the World Bank **AGILE** project.
3. **PLASU Bokkos, Scholarships & Sports Stadiums**:
   - Accreditation and infrastructure upgrades at **Plateau State University (PLASU) Bokkos, College of Education Gindiri, College of Health Tech Zawan & Pankshin, and CAST Kurgwi**, upward review of **student bursaries and Nigerian Law School grants**, remodeling of **Rwang Pam Township Stadium**, and ongoing works at **Zaria Road New Jos International Stadium**.

## 6. Water, Clean Energy, ICT, Hospitality & Environment (PSWB, PSEC, PICTDA, PEPSA, ACReSAL & MTC&H)
1. **Yelwa, Lamingo & Yakubu Gowon Dam Water Treatment Plants**:
   - Rehabilitated and commissioned the **Yelwa Water Treatment Plant** restoring clean pipe-borne water to **Bukuru, Gyel, and neighboring communities**, alongside overhauls at **Lamingo and Yakubu Gowon Dam** water treatment plants and SURWASH solar boreholes.
2. **Rural Electrification & 50kW Qua'an Pan Solar Mini-Grid (PSEC)**:
   - Enacted the Plateau State Electricity Law and deployed decentralized renewable mini-grids, including the **50-kilowatt solar mini-grid in Qua'an Pan LGA**, powering rural communities, PHCs, and schools.
3. **2,500km Broadband Fibre (PICTDA), Code Plateau 6.0, PLAGIS & AI Contact Centre**:
   - PPP with **Solutron** expanding broadband fibre to **2,500 km**, training thousands of youths via **Code Plateau 6.0 & 3MTT**, launching **PLAGIS e-C of O** with a 50% waiver, and launching the **08000000456 AI Citizen Contact Centre**.
4. **PEPSA Refuse Fleet, ACReSAL Erosion Control & Tourism Revival**:
   - New PEPSA compactor trucks clearing urban waste dumps, World Bank **ACReSAL** gully-erosion control, PPP revitalization of **Hill Station Hotel, Plateau Hotel, and Jos Wildlife Park**, and **ONE PLATEAU FEST / December Tourism Month**.

## 7. Civil Service Welfare, Security, IGR & 2024–2026 Budgets (MOF, PSIRS, OHCS, Operation Rainbow & PLASIEC)
1. **₦70,000 Minimum Wage, Salary/Pension Arrears Cleared & Gratuities Resumed**:
   - Cleared 4–6 months of inherited salary arrears, pays monthly salaries on or before the 25th, resumed pension/gratuity payments after years of stagnation, and implemented the **₦70,000 New National Minimum Wage**.
2. **600+ Operation Rainbow Operatives, OPEP Support & Peaceful LGA Elections**:
   - Recruited and kitted **600+ Operation Rainbow operatives and Agro-Rangers** with patrol vehicles and a 24/7 toll-free control room (**0800 0022 3344** / **09064000112**), supported **Operation Enduring Peace (OPEP)**, and conducted peaceful elections across all **17 LGAs and 325 wards on October 9, 2024**.
3. **IGR Growth & Transformational Budgets (2024, 2025 & 2026)**:
   - **PSIRS Digital CBS**: Grew IGR from ~₦15B to **₦38.36B in 2024** and **₦62.54B target in 2025/2026**.
   - **2024 Budget ("Budget of New Beginnings")**: ₦314.86 Billion.
   - **2025 Budget ("Budget of Sustainability and Economic Transformation")**: ₦468.49 Billion (60.9% Capital Expenditure).
   - **2026 Budget ("Budget of Consolidation and Sustainability 2.0")**: Scales up capital expenditure for road completion, healthcare, agriculture, education, water, and security.
