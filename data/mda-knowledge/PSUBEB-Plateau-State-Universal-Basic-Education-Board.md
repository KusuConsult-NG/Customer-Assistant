# PSUBEB — Plateau State Universal Basic Education Board
Also known as: Plateau State Universal Basic Education Board (PSUBEB), SUBEB Plateau

## Current Leadership
- **Executive Chairman**: Hon. Sunday Amuna
- **Role Overview**: Leading public primary and junior secondary education revitalisation and school infrastructure.

## Past Leadership & Administrative History
- **Former Executive Chairman**: Prof. Matthew Sule (2015 – 2023 (Lalong administration))

Formed under the Compulsory, Free Universal Basic Education Act 2004 to administer public primary schools and junior secondary schools.

## Mandate and Core Functions
Manage public primary and junior secondary schools across all 17 LGAs; disburse and manage UBE matching grants; construct and renovate classrooms; recruit, train, and pay basic education teachers; and distribute free textbooks.

**Vision**: Every Plateau child of school age in school, acquiring qualitative, foundational literacy, numeracy, and life skills.

**Mission**: To eliminate out-of-school children, provide child-friendly school environments, and ensure qualitative foundational learning through motivated basic school teachers.

## Key Departments and Directorates
- Department of Physical Planning & School Infrastructure
- Department of Academic Services, Quality Assurance & Curriculum
- Department of Teacher Professional Development
- Department of Social Mobilization & Enrolment Drive
- Department of Planning, Research & Statistics

## Public Services and Citizen Assistance
- Public primary and junior secondary school enrollment for children aged 6 to 15
- Reporting dilapidated school classrooms, storm damages, or lack of water/toilet facilities
- Primary school teacher recruitment, promotion, and payroll administration via Local Education Authorities (LEAs)
- Distribution of free instructional materials, mathematical sets, and textbooks

## Current Projects, Strategic Initiatives & Ongoing Activities
- Digital Classrooms & Smart Schools Pilot: Equipping model public primary schools with interactive digital whiteboards, solar-powered computer labs, and teacher tablet devices.
- Universal Basic Education Infrastructure Upgrade: Constructing and rehabilitating modern classrooms, perimeter fences, and sanitation facilities across primary schools in all 17 LGAs.
