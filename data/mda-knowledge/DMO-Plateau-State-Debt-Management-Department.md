# DMO — Plateau State Debt Management Department
Also known as: Plateau state Debt Management Department (DMO), Plateau DMO

## Current Leadership
- **Director General**: Mrs. Atihong Yengoet
- **Role Overview**: Leading public debt portfolio recording, debt sustainability analysis, and loan servicing.

## Past Leadership & Administrative History
- **Former Director General**: Mr. Emmanuel Rurum (Pre-2023)

Responsible for recording, servicing, and reporting all domestic and external public debt portfolios of the Plateau State Government.

## Mandate and Core Functions
Maintain a comprehensive database of all state public debts, loans, guarantees, and contractual liabilities; conduct debt sustainability analyses; advise on borrowing limits; and ensure timely servicing of public debts.

**Vision**: Prudent, transparent, and sustainable public debt management that supports strategic state infrastructure without compromising fiscal stability.

**Mission**: To manage Plateau State's debt obligations cost-effectively and transparently, adhering strictly to international best practices and fiscal responsibility thresholds.

## Key Departments and Directorates
- Department of Debt Recording & Settlement
- Department of Debt Strategy & Risk Analysis
- Department of Monitoring, Policy & Legal Advisory

## Public Services and Citizen Assistance
- Quarterly and annual publication of official Plateau State public debt statistical reports
- Verification of state debt figures and domestic contractor loan liability schedules
- Debt sustainability analysis consultations with civil society and financial institutions
