# December Tourism Month 2026 — Plateau State Events Lineup (November 2026 to January 2027)
Also known as: Tourism Month, December Tourism Month, ONE PLATEAU FEST, Christmas events in Jos, festive season events in Plateau State

December is Plateau State's official Tourism Month, a policy initiative of His Excellency, Governor Caleb Mutfwang. This year the State's public and private sector events are coordinated under ONE PLATEAU FEST, so that visitors and residents can enjoy a single, well-planned season from the Unity Carols to the New Year Crossover. The Plateau State Tourism Corporation (PSTC) coordinates the State's events.

## Events, in date order

- **19 November 2026 — 2nd Plateau Youth Tourism Roundtable** (youth enterprise, digital tourism and jobs). Venue: Jos. Organised by: the Government (Plateau State Tourism Corporation, PSTC) with UNDP.
- **20 to 22 November 2026 — TinCity Fashion Week.** Venue: Jos. Organised by: the private sector.
- **22 November 2026 — ONE PLATEAU FEST launch.** Venue: Secretariat Bridge, Jos. Organised by: ONE PLATEAU FEST partners with the Plateau State Tourism Corporation (PSTC).
- **22 November to 1 December 2026 — Enchanting Plateau Exhibition** (Plateau products, crafts and businesses). Venue: Secretariat Junction, Jos. Organised by: the private sector.
- **Late November 2026, dates to be announced — Unity Carols and Praise Festival** (a three-day opening of the festive season). Venue: Ten Commandments Prayer Ground, Du. Organised by: the Government (the State).
- **1 December 2026 — PRTV Christmas Tree Lighting and opening of the Secretariat Bridge Beautification.** Venue: Secretariat Bridge, Jos. Organised by: the Government (Plateau Radio Television Corporation, PRTV, and the Plateau State Tourism Corporation, PSTC).
- **10 to 12 December 2026 — Plateau Cultural Carnival** (parades, troupes and cultural showcases). Venue: Jos, venue to be announced. Organised by: jointly, public and private sector.
- **18 to 28 December 2026 — Jos Family Christmas Festival** (Christmas Village with an opening concert, Christmas Market, Children's Funfair, Little Miss Tourism, theatre and nativity). Venue: Christmas Village, Jonah Jang Way, Jos. Organised by: the Government (Plateau State Tourism Corporation, PSTC), with private partners.
- **18 to 19 December 2026 — Jos Food Festival.** Venue: Christmas Market, Jos. Organised by: the private sector.
- **19 December 2026, to be confirmed — Plateau Unity Lunch.** Venue: Jos, route to be announced. Organised by: the private sector.
- **About 20 to 22 December 2026 — ONE Movie Fest.** Venue: Jos. Organised by: the private sector.
- **22 to 27 December 2026 — Urban Fest** (graffiti, music, basketball and a gaming arena). Venue: Azi Nyako Youth Centre, Jos. Organised by: the private sector, supported by the Plateau State Tourism Corporation (PSTC).
- **31 December 2026 to 1 January 2027 — New Year Crossover and Fireworks Countdown.** Venue: Secretariat Flyover Bridge and roundabout, Jos. Organised by: the Government (Plateau State Tourism Corporation, PSTC).

## Looking ahead

- **March 2027 — PLAFEST**, the State's flagship culture, tourism and creative economy festival.

## Please note

Dates and venues may change. Where a date or venue says "to be announced" or "to be confirmed", it has not been fixed yet: say so, and do not guess one. Visitors should check official Plateau State Government and Plateau State Tourism Corporation (PSTC) channels for updates.
