Also known as: Ministry of Lands and Survey, Ministry of Lands, Survey and Town Planning

Operates the Plateau Geographic Information System (PLAGIS) for electronic land titling and cadastral management.

## Mandate and Core Functions
Administer all state land resources, conduct cadastral and geodetic surveys, issue Certificates of Occupancy (C of O), manage Deeds Registry, formulate town and regional plans, and prevent land encroachments.

**Vision**: An efficient, transparent, and digitally driven land administration system unlocking capital value and promoting orderly spatial development.

**Mission**: To secure property ownership rights through automated cadastral mapping, seamless land titling, and strict enforcement of sustainable town planning masterplans.

## Key Departments and Directorates
- Department of Lands Administration
- Department of Cadastral & Geodetic Surveys
- Department of Town & Regional Planning
- Department of Deeds & Title Registration
- Plateau Geographic Information System (PLAGIS) Unit

## Public Services and Citizen Assistance
- Processing and issuance of Statutory Certificates of Occupancy (C of O) for residential, commercial, and agricultural lands
- Governor's Consent processing for mortgages, deeds of assignment, leases, and property transfers
- Official land registry searches, boundary beacons verification, and cadastral survey charting
- Town planning layout approvals and building setback inspection

## Current Projects, Strategic Initiatives & Ongoing Activities
- PLAGIS Digital Land Administration Reform: Plateau State Geographic Information System digitalization of all cadastral maps, automated land title searches, electronic land registries, and accelerated issuance of electronic Certificates of Occupancy (e-C of O).
- State-Wide Aerial Cadastral Mapping: High-resolution GIS aerial mapping of urban and rural land parcels to resolve boundary disputes and enhance land documentation.
