# PADP — Plateau Agricultural Development Programme
Also known as: Plateau Agricultural Development Programme (PADP), PADP Jos

## Current Leadership
- **Programme Manager**: Mr. Bitrus Dalyop
- **Role Overview**: Directing statewide agricultural extension delivery, smallholder farmer support, and demonstration farms.

## Past Leadership & Administrative History
- Predecessor leadership records maintained under historical state gazettes and executive transition archives.

Established under World Bank counterpart funding to provide front-line agricultural extension services to smallholder farmers.

## Mandate and Core Functions
Deliver agricultural extension advisory to smallholder farmers; train farmers on climate-smart agronomic practices; introduce high-yield certified seeds and seedlings; manage farm demonstration plots; and monitor pest attacks.

**Vision**: A food-secure Plateau State powered by empowered, knowledgeable, and technologically equipped smallholder farmers.

**Mission**: To bridge the gap between agricultural research and farming practices through effective extension services.

## Key Departments and Directorates
- Department of Extension Services & Farmer Training
- Department of Technical Services & Seed Multiplication
- Department of Rural Infrastructure & Fadama Development
- Department of Monitoring, Evaluation & Agronomic Surveys

## Public Services and Citizen Assistance
- Field extension advisory visits by Village Extension Workers (VEWs) across all agricultural zones
- Provision of certified improved crop seeds (maize, Irish potato, cowpea, sorghum, rice)
- Training on integrated pest management (Fall Armyworm control, potato blight prevention)
- Farmer Field School (FFS) demonstration classes and modern irrigation techniques
