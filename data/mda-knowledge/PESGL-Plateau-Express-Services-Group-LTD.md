# PESGL — Plateau Express Services Group LTD
Also known as: Plateau Express Services Group LTD (PESGL), Plateau Riders, Plateau Express Services

## Current Leadership
- **Managing Director / CEO**: Hon. Samuel Jok Gwott
- **Role Overview**: Leading modern fleet expansion, inter-state route revitalization, and Tin City Metro urban bus services.

## Past Leadership & Administrative History
- **Former General Manager**: Hon. Samuel Jok Gwott (2019 – 2023)

Originally established as Plateau Riders in 1989. Restructured into a modern logistics and multimodal transport corporate group.

## Mandate and Core Functions
Provide safe, reliable, and affordable commercial inter-state passenger transit, urban intra-city mass transit, cargo haulage, and courier logistics services.

**Vision**: To be Nigeria's safest, most innovative, and technologically integrated state-owned transport and logistics brand.

**Mission**: To deliver exceptional customer travel experiences, link major Nigerian commercial hubs, and provide subsidized mass transit for Plateau citizens.

## Key Departments and Directorates
- Inter-State Passenger Operations
- Tin City Metro Urban Transit Operations
- Logistics, Courier & Haulage Services
- Fleet Maintenance & Technical Workshop
- Customer Care & Digital Ticketing

## Public Services and Citizen Assistance
- Inter-state passenger express travel from Jos to Abuja, Lagos, Kano, Port Harcourt, Kaduna, Bauchi, Enugu, etc.
- Tin City Metro subsidized intra-city public bus rides across Jos-Bukuru metropolitan corridors
- Waybill, parcel delivery, and express courier logistics across Nigeria
- Automated online bus seat booking and digital ticket reservation

## Current Projects, Strategic Initiatives & Ongoing Activities
- Tin City Metro Bus Transit Modernization: Deployment of 15 German-engineered energy-efficient metro buses operating along 8 major Jos-Bukuru corridors with automated digital smart card ticketing.
- Plateau Riders Interstate Fleet Modernization: Fleet expansion with new air-conditioned passenger buses, online seat reservations, and digitized logistics courier delivery.
