# OAG — Office of the Accountant General
Also known as: Office of the Accountant General (OAG), Accountant General's Office

## Current Leadership
- **Accountant General**: Mrs. Naanret A. Manset
- **Role Overview**: Chief Accounting Officer of receipts and disbursements of the Plateau State Government.

## Past Leadership & Administrative History
- **Former Accountant General**: Mr. Cyril Tsenyil (2015 – 2023 (Lalong administration))

Custodians of the state treasury, operating the electronic central payment platform and statutory accounting records.

## Mandate and Core Functions
Serve as Chief Accounting Officer of receipts and payments of the state, produce annual financial statements of the government, manage state bank accounts and cash flows, and enforce public financial regulations.

**Vision**: A transparent, digital, and world-class state treasury delivering accurate, real-time financial stewardship.

**Mission**: To maintain the highest professional standards in accounting, revenue tracking, and public expenditure reporting for Plateau State.

## Key Departments and Directorates
- Treasury Operations & Cash Management
- Final Accounts & Financial Reporting
- Payroll & Electronic Payments Administration
- Inspectorate & Internal Financial Control
- Funds Management & Investments

## Public Services and Citizen Assistance
- Central electronic salary and pension payments processing and remittance inquiries
- Verification of government revenue receipts and e-payment confirmations
- Publication of annual statutory audited financial statements and SFTAS public accounts reports
