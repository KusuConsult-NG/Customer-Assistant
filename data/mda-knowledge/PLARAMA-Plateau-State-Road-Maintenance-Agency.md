# PLARAMA — Plateau State Road Maintenance Agency
Also known as: Plateau State Road Maintenance Agency (PLARAMA), PLARAMA

## Current Leadership
- **General Manager**: Engr. Pam Dung
- **Role Overview**: Leading rapid response pothole patching, asphalt maintenance, and drainage clearing.

## Past Leadership & Administrative History
- Predecessor leadership records maintained under historical state gazettes and executive transition archives.

Dedicated road maintenance agency operating under the supervision of the Ministry of Works to keep state highways motorable.

## Mandate and Core Functions
Carry out routine and periodic road maintenance, emergency pothole repairs, culvert restorations, and storm drain clearings on all state roads across the 17 LGAs.

**Vision**: Zero pothole highways and seamless, safe vehicular mobility across Plateau State.

**Mission**: To maintain state roads efficiently and proactively using skilled direct-labor teams and quality asphalt materials.

## Key Departments and Directorates
- Department of Direct Labour Operations & Asphalt Patching
- Department of Road Drainage & Structural Maintenance
- Department of Highway Monitoring & Emergency Intervention

## Public Services and Citizen Assistance
- Rapid intervention pothole patching on damaged township and inter-town state roads
- Desilting and rehabilitation of road culverts to prevent seasonal flooding
- Public reporting intake for dangerous road washouts and damaged bridges
