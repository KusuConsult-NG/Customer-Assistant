# PMDC — Plateau Minerals Development Company Limited
Also known as: Plateau Minerals Development Company Limited (PMDC), Plateau Minerals Development Company

## Current Leadership
- **Managing Director**: Mr. Matthews Dawap
- **Role Overview**: Leading state solid mineral exploration, joint ventures, and mineral beneficiation.

## Past Leadership & Administrative History
- **Former Managing Director**: Mr. Gyang Dalyop (Pre-2023)

The state-owned enterprise established to commercially explore, process, and partner in the development of solid mineral deposits.

## Mandate and Core Functions
Engage in commercial mining exploration, joint venture partnerships, mineral processing, and value-addition for tin, columbite, zircon, tantalite, lead, zinc, and barites abundant in Plateau State.

**Vision**: To be Nigeria's foremost state-owned commercial mining corporation unlocking sustainable mineral wealth.

**Mission**: To conduct responsible, environmentally friendly, and profitable commercial mining operations while empowering host communities.

## Key Departments and Directorates
- Department of Geology & Mineral Exploration
- Department of Joint Ventures, Legal & Business Development
- Department of Mineral Beneficiation & Processing Operations
- Department of Community Relations & Environmental Safety

## Public Services and Citizen Assistance
- Joint-venture partnerships with local and international mining investors
- Mineral laboratory assaying, sample testing, and purity certification
- Artisanal mining cooperative organization, safety training, and formal market linkages
