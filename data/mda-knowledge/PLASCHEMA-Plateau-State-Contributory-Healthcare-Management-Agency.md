# PLASCHEMA — Plateau State Contributory Healthcare Management Agency
Also known as: Plateau State Contributory Healthcare Management Agency (PLASCHEMA), Plateau State Contributory Health Insurance Management Agency, PLASCHEMA

## Current Leadership
- **Director General**: Dr. Dawal Solomon Kwande
- **Role Overview**: Appointed by Governor Caleb Mutfwang. Leads universal health coverage expansion, BHCPF implementation, informal-sector enrollment, and facility accreditation.

## Past Leadership & Administrative History
- **Former Director General**: Dr. Fabong Jemchang Yildam (2019 – 2023 (Founding Director General))

Established under the PLASCHEMA Law 2019 to achieve universal health coverage for all Plateau residents, reducing out-of-pocket medical expenditures.

## Mandate and Core Functions
Implement mandatory and voluntary contributory health insurance in Plateau State; accredit primary, secondary, and tertiary healthcare providers; manage benefit packages; and disburse equity health funds to vulnerable citizens under the Basic Health Care Provision Fund (BHCPF).

**Vision**: A healthy Plateau State where every citizen, regardless of economic status, has financial risk protection against catastrophic healthcare expenditures.

**Mission**: To provide equitable, affordable, and quality healthcare services through an efficient, transparent, and technology-driven contributory health insurance scheme.

## Public Services and Citizen Assistance
- Formal Sector Health Plan: Public civil servants and organized private sector employee enrollments (5% basic salary)
- Informal Sector Health Plan: Artisans, traders, farmers, transport operators, and self-employed individuals (₦12,000/year per person; ₦50,000/year family of 6)
- Equity Plan (Vulnerable): Free government and BHCPF-subsidized healthcare coverage for pregnant women, children under 5, elderly aged 65+, and persons living with disabilities
- Healthcare provider facility selection, biometric enrollment, and e-card issuance
- Complaints and grievance resolution against accredited hospitals charging unauthorized fees or denying covered medicines (Helpline: 0700-700-1111)

## Current Projects, Strategic Initiatives & Ongoing Activities
- Informal Sector Universal Health Coverage Expansion: Massive statewide enrollment drives enrolling artisans, market traders, farmers, and self-employed individuals at ₦12,000/year per person or ₦50,000/year for families of 6.
- Retiree Free Healthcare Scheme: Providing fully subsidized medical care coverage for state and local government retirees under Governor Caleb Mutfwang's social welfare mandate.
- Basic Health Care Provision Fund (BHCPF) Equity Plan: Free healthcare access for vulnerable groups (pregnant women, children under 5, elderly 65+, and persons living with disabilities).
- Digital Health Management Information System: Hospital biometric verification, automated provider claims processing, and electronic patient authentication across accredited healthcare centers.
