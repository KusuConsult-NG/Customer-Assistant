# MoBEP — Ministry of Budget and Economic Planning
Also known as: Ministry of Budget and Planning

## Current Leadership
- **Commissioner**: Hon. Adams Bulus Lekshak
- **Permanent Secretary**: Mrs. Martha Azi
- **Role Overview**: Reassigned from Ministry of Works during cabinet reshuffle to lead economic planning and budget coordination.

## Past Leadership & Administrative History
- **Former Commissioner**: Hon. Chrysanthus Dawam (August 2023 – February 2025 (Mutfwang cabinet))
- **Former Commissioner**: Hon. Sylvester Wallangko (2015 – 2023 (Lalong administration))
- **Former Commissioner**: Mr. Ezekiel Gomos (Pre-2015)

Serves as the think-tank of the state government, formulating Medium-Term Expenditure Frameworks (MTEF) and State Development Plans. Coordinates Open Government Partnership (OGP) commitments.

## Mandate and Core Functions
Formulating macroeconomic policies, preparing annual state capital and recurrent budgets, tracking budgetary allocations, monitoring state projects, and coordinating with multilateral development partners (World Bank, AfDB, UNDP, UNICEF).

**Vision**: A model state planning agency driving transparent, equitable, and sustainable socioeconomic growth for Plateau State.

**Mission**: To ensure prudent allocation of state resources through participatory, transparent budgeting and rigorous evidence-based monitoring and evaluation.

## Key Departments and Directorates
- Department of Budget Formulation & Monitoring
- Department of Macroeconomic Planning & Research
- Department of International Cooperation & Donor Coordination
- Department of Monitoring & Evaluation (M&E)
- Department of Administration & Human Resources

## Public Services and Citizen Assistance
- Hosting public citizen budget consultation sessions across the three senatorial zones
- Publishing approved annual budgets, budget performance reports, and citizen budget summaries
- Facilitating Open Government Partnership (OGP) public commitments and citizen feedback mechanisms
- Monitoring capital development projects and addressing citizen expenditure inquiries

## Supervised Parastatals and Tertiary Institutions
- Plateau State Bureau of Statistics (PSBS)
- Plateau State Sustainable Development Goals Project Support Unit (SDGs-PSU)
