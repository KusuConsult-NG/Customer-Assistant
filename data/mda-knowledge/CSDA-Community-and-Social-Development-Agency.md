# CSDA — Community and Social Development Agency
Also known as: Community and Social Development Agency (CSDA), Plateau CSDA

## Current Leadership
- **General Manager**: Mr. Dauda D. Dinju
- **Role Overview**: Leading community-driven development micro-projects across poor rural communities.

## Past Leadership & Administrative History
- **Former General Manager**: Mr. Daniel Chup (Pre-2023)

Created to manage community-driven development projects funded through tripartite partnerships between the World Bank, Federal Government, and Plateau State Government.

## Mandate and Core Functions
Empower poor rural communities to plan, co-finance, construct, and maintain their own priority social infrastructure micro-projects in health, water, education, and rural access.

**Vision**: Self-reliant rural communities actively driving their own sustainable socioeconomic development.

**Mission**: To build community capacity, reduce rural poverty, and improve access of poor households to basic social services.

## Key Departments and Directorates
- Department of Operations & Community Mobilization
- Department of Monitoring & Evaluation
- Department of Finance & Administration
- Department of Engineering & Project Quality Control

## Public Services and Citizen Assistance
- Community-Driven Development (CDD) micro-grants for rural community water schemes, clinic blocks, and culverts
- Community Project Management Committee (CPMC) training on procurement and project maintenance
- Vulnerable group empowerment projects for widows, elderly, and persons with disabilities
