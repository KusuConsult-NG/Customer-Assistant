## 4. PLASCHEMA (Health Insurance) Schemes, Rates & Hospitals

The Plateau State Contributory Healthcare Management Agency (PLASCHEMA) is established under Plateau State Law No. 7 of 2019 to provide universal, affordable, and high-quality healthcare coverage to every resident of the state.

### Director-General
- **Current DG**: Dr. Dawal Solomon Kwande (appointed by Governor Caleb Mutfwang).
- **Headquarters**: Plot 14, Joseph Gomwalk Road, Jos, Plateau State.

### Health Insurance Plans & Premium Pricing
1. **Formal Sector Plan**:
   - Covers all employees in Plateau State Civil Service and 17 Local Government Councils.
   - Funded by a 5% monthly deduction (combined government employer and employee payroll contribution).
   - Covers principal, spouse, and up to 4 biological children under 18 years.
2. **Informal Sector Plan / Community-Based Health Insurance (CBHIS)**:
   - For market traders, farmers, artisans, entrepreneurs, and self-employed residents.
   - **Individual Premium**: **₦12,000 per year** (equivalent to ₦1,000/month).
   - **Family Premium**: **₦50,000 per year** (covers husband, wife, and up to 4 children).
   - Instant activation upon registration and payment at any PLASCHEMA registration desk or accredited bank.
3. **Vulnerable Group Social Health Insurance Scheme (VGSHIS) / BHCPF**:
   - **100% Free / Fully Subsidized** by the Plateau State Government and the Federal Basic Health Care Provision Fund.
   - Target beneficiaries: Pregnant women (covers all antenatal visits, delivery, and postpartum care), children under 5 years, persons aged 65 and above, persons living with disabilities (PWDs), and poorest of the poor in rural wards.
4. **Students Health Insurance Scheme (TSHIP)**:
   - Covers tertiary students in PLASU Bokkos, PLAPOLY, College of Education Gindiri, CAST Kurgwi, and health colleges at ₦3,000 to ₦5,000 per academic session.

### What PLASCHEMA Covers (Benefits Package)
- General outpatient consultations and specialist referrals.
- Essential generic drugs and pharmaceuticals from accredited pharmacies.
- Laboratory diagnostic investigations (blood, urine, ultrasound, X-ray).
- Maternal and child healthcare: Antenatal care, vaginal delivery, and Cesarean section (C-Section).
- Surgeries: Appendectomy, hernia repair, wound debridement, cataract extraction, and emergency trauma surgery.
- Hospital admissions: Up to 21 cumulative days per year in general ward.
- Dental care: Pain management, extractions, and fillings.
- Eye care: Refraction, visual acuity tests, and prescription corrective lenses.

### Key Accredited Hospitals by Senatorial Zone
- **Jos North**: Plateau Specialist Hospital, Bingham University Teaching Hospital (BHUTH), Jos University Teaching Hospital (JUTH), Our Lady of Apostles (OLA) Hospital, Faith Alive Hospital, Evangel Hospital.
- **Jos South**: Vom Christian Hospital, General Hospital Bukuru, Zawan Catholic Hospital.
- **Barkin Ladi**: General Hospital Barkin Ladi, Seventh-Day Adventist Hospital.

## 5. Higher Education & Tertiary Institutions
1. **Plateau State University (PLASU), Bokkos**
   - State-owned university established in 2005. Offers degrees in Natural & Applied Sciences, Social Sciences, Management, Arts, Environmental Sciences, and Law.
   - **Vice Chancellor**: Prof. Shedrack Best.
2. **Plateau State Polytechnic (PLAPOLY), Barkin Ladi**
   - Main Campus: Barkin Ladi; City Campus: Jos. Offers ND and HND in Engineering, Science Laboratory Technology, Accountancy, Business Administration, Computer Science, and Public Admin.
   - **Rector**: Dr. Clement Chirman.
3. **College of Education, Gindiri (Mangu LGA)**
   - Premier teacher training institution awarding NCE (Nigeria Certificate in Education) and degree programs affiliated with University of Jos.
4. **College of Health Technology, Zawan (Jos South LGA)**
   - Trains Community Health Extension Workers (CHEWs), Medical Lab Technicians, Health Information Managers, and Environmental Health Officers.
5. **College of Health Technology, Pankshin (Pankshin LGA)**
   - Specialized health paramedical and clinical training institution.
6. **College of Arts, Science and Technology (CAST), Kurgwi (Qua'an Pan LGA)**
   - National IJMB examination center of excellence and diploma-awarding institution.
   - **Provost**: Mr. Birmiap Patrick Yilpoe.
7. **Plateau State Scholarship Board**
   - Mandate: Annual bursary awards to indigent students of Plateau State origin in accredited universities, polytechnics, and colleges. Special sponsorship for Nigerian Law School candidates and overseas bilateral exchange scholars.

## 6. Key Public Infrastructure & Flagship Projects
1. **Tin City Metro Urban Transit Scheme**
   - 14 high-capacity luxury Neoplan buses deployed across the Greater Jos-Bukuru metropolitan masterplan.
   - Features digital smart ticketing (Tin City Metro Card). Flat fare ₦200 per route, significantly reducing commuter costs.
   - Operates between Terminus, British America, Bukuru, Rayfield, Maraban Jama'a, Tudun Wada, and Farin Gada.
2. **Jos Main Market (Terminus Market) Reconstruction**
   - Full urban renewal and redevelopment project restoring the historic multi-billion naira commercial center destroyed in 2002.
3. **Plateau State Geographic Information System (PLAGIS)**
   - Modernized digital land titling, electronic Certificate of Occupancy (e-C of O) processing, search reports, and deed assignments under Ministry of Lands, Survey & Town Planning.
4. **PICTDA Digital Initiatives**
   - **Code Plateau**: Software development fellowship training youth in frontend, backend, UI/UX, and cloud engineering.
   - **3MTT**: Three Million Technical Talent training in partnership with the Federal Ministry of Communications, Innovation and Digital Economy.
   - **2,500km Fiber Optic Network**: State-wide broadband expansion connecting all 17 LGA secretariats and educational hubs.
5. **Agriculture & Smallholder Support**
   - Distribution of over 200 truckloads of subsidized chemical fertilizers (NPK and Urea) directly to registered farmers across all 17 LGAs.
   - Reactivation of the Agricultural Services and Training Centre (ASTC) tractor hiring services.
   - Irish Potato Value Chain Enhancement to curb bacterial wilt in Bokkos, Mangu, and Barkin Ladi.
