# JMDB — Jos Metropolitan Development Board
Also known as: The Jos Metropolitan Development Board (JMDB), Jos Metropolitan Development Board

## Current Leadership
- **General Manager**: Arc. Hart Bankat
- **Role Overview**: Leading Greater Jos urban regeneration, development control, and environmental beautification.

## Past Leadership & Administrative History
- **Former General Manager**: Arc. Sukukum Burdap (2019 – 2023 (Lalong administration))
- **Former General Manager**: Mr. Mathias Hata (2015 – 2019 (Lalong administration))

Established in 1974 to oversee orderly urban growth of the Jos-Bukuru metropolis across Jos North, Jos South, Jos East, and Bassa LGAs.

## Mandate and Core Functions
Enforce development control and urban building codes, approve architectural and structural building drawings, prevent illegal construction and encroachments, maintain city roads, drainages, and streetlights, and regulate outdoor advertising.

**Vision**: A functional, clean, aesthetically pleasing, and resilient metropolitan city planned to international standards.

**Mission**: To enforce smart urban planning, maintain modern public infrastructure, and deliver efficient city municipal services across Greater Jos.

## Key Departments and Directorates
- Department of Development Control & Inspections
- Department of Urban Planning, Architecture & Design
- Department of Civil & Structural Engineering
- Department of Environmental Sanitation & City Greening
- Department of Outdoor Advertising & Signage Regulations

## Public Services and Citizen Assistance
- Building plan assessments, structural approvals, and construction permits for residential and commercial buildings
- Right-of-way compliance verification and demolition of hazardous or unauthorized structures
- Clearing blocked city arterial drainages and stormwater channels
- Outdoor advertising billboard registration, positioning permits, and signage control
- Reporting illegal building construction and right-of-way encroachments

## Current Projects, Strategic Initiatives & Ongoing Activities
- Greater Jos Master Plan GIS Revision: Digital zoning and urban master plan overhaul to manage urban growth, protect green areas, and guide sustainable metropolitan development.
- E-Permit Digital Building Approval Portal: Online portal for submitting, vetting, and issuing building approvals, structural safety certificates, and architectural clearances.
- Urban Renewal & Metropolitan Sanitation Enforcement: Street lighting restoration, clearing unauthorized roadside setbacks, modernizing traffic intersections, and urban beautification in Jos-Bukuru.
