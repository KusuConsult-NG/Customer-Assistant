# PSIRS — Plateau State Internal Revenue Service
Also known as: Plateau State Internal Revenue Service (PSIRS), Plateau Board of Internal Revenue

## Current Leadership
- **Executive Chairman**: Dr. Jim Pam Wayas
- **Role Overview**: Leading automated tax collection, taxpayer enumeration, and voluntary tax compliance.

## Past Leadership & Administrative History
- **Former Executive Chairman**: Mr. Dashe Arlat (2015 – 2023 (Lalong administration))

The sole statutory tax administration body of Plateau State, operating autonomous tax collection under the Plateau State Revenue Law.

## Mandate and Core Functions
Assess, collect, and account for all internally generated revenue (IGR), personal income taxes, stamp duties, withholding taxes, road taxes, and capital gains tax; maintain the State Taxpayer Database; and combat tax evasion.

**Vision**: A transparent, taxpayer-friendly, and fully automated revenue service maximizing Plateau State financial autonomy.

**Mission**: To mobilize domestic revenue efficiently through fair tax administration, digital tax payment gateways, and transparent accounting.

## Key Departments and Directorates
- Department of Personal Income Tax & PAYE
- Department of Direct Assessment & Informal Sector Taxes
- Department of Road Taxes & Motor Licensing
- Department of Tax Audit, Enforcement & Investigations
- Department of Information Technology & Electronic Billing

## Public Services and Citizen Assistance
- Issuance of State Taxpayer Identification Number (Plateau TIN) for individuals and registered businesses
- Application, assessment, and issuance of electronic Tax Clearance Certificates (e-TCC)
- Filing annual Pay-As-You-Earn (PAYE) returns for employers of labour and private companies
- Motor vehicle registration, road tax, new vehicle number plates, and driver license endorsements
- Direct online tax assessment and instant electronic receipting

## Current Projects, Strategic Initiatives & Ongoing Activities
- Central Billing System (CBS) & Automated Tax Clearance Certificate (e-TCC): Fully automated taxpayer self-service portal enabling online tax filing, instant electronic receipts, and automated tax clearance verification.
- Harmonized Informal Sector Revenue Framework: Digital ticketing and single-point collection system eliminating duplicate taxes for market traders, artisans, and transport operators across the 17 LGAs.
