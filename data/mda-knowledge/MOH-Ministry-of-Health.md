# MOH — Ministry of Health
Also known as: Plateau State Ministry of Health (MOH)

## Current Leadership
- **Commissioner for Health**: Dr. Nicolas Baamlong
- **Permanent Secretary**: Dr. Cletus Bako / Dr. Agabus
- **Role Overview**: Appointed during cabinet restructuring to lead primary and secondary health sector renewal.

## Past Leadership & Administrative History
- **Former Commissioner**: Dr. Cletus Bako Shurkuk (August 2023 – February 2025 (reassigned to MOSTI))
- **Former Commissioner**: Dr. Nimkong Lar (2019 – 2023 (Lalong administration))
- **Former Commissioner**: Dr. Kunde Deyin (2015 – 2019 (Lalong administration))
- **Former Commissioner**: Dr. Fahat Fom (2007 – 2015 (Jang administration))

Oversees the state healthcare architecture. In recent years established PLASCHEMA for health insurance and PSDMCMA for pooled drug procurement.

## Mandate and Core Functions
Formulate, regulate, and supervise state healthcare policy; control epidemics and infectious diseases; regulate private and public healthcare facilities; ensure universal health coverage; and develop health human resources.

**Vision**: A healthy, prosperous Plateau State where every citizen has guaranteed access to affordable, quality, and equitable healthcare.

**Mission**: To deliver comprehensive, patient-centred, and sustainable preventive, curative, and rehabilitative health services across all 17 LGAs.

## Key Departments and Directorates
- Department of Public Health & Disease Control
- Department of Medical Services & Hospital Standards
- Department of Nursing Services
- Department of Food, Drugs & Pharmaceutical Services
- Department of Health Planning, Research & Statistics
- Department of Primary Healthcare Liaison

## Public Services and Citizen Assistance
- Licensing, accreditation, and annual inspection of private clinics, maternity homes, and diagnostic centres
- Epidemic outbreak response, surveillance, and toll-free reporting (Cholera, Lassa Fever, Measles, Mpox)
- Statewide maternal, newborn, and child health healthcare interventions and routine immunization
- Medical outreach missions and surgical camps for underserved rural communities
- Accreditation and clinical training approvals for health professional interns
