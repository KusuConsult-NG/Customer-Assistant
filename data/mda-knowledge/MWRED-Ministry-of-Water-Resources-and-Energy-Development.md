# MWRED — Ministry of Water Resources and Energy Development
Also known as: Ministry of Water Resources, Ministry of Water Resources and Energy

## Current Leadership
- **Commissioner**: Hon. Bashir Lawandi Datti
- **Role Overview**: Reassigned from Ministry of Youth and Sports Development during cabinet restructuring.

## Past Leadership & Administrative History
- **Former Commissioner**: Hon. Noel Nkup (August 2023 – February 2025 (Mutfwang cabinet))
- **Former Commissioner**: Engr. Saad Bello (2019 – 2023 (Lalong administration))
- **Former Commissioner**: Hon. David Wuyep (2015 – 2019 (Lalong administration))
- **Former Commissioner**: Hon. Idi Waziri (2007 – 2011 (Jang administration))

Combines urban water treatment and distribution with rural water schemes and energy/power generation development.

## Mandate and Core Functions
Formulate water and energy policies, rehabilitate water treatment plants and dams, expand piped water networks, regulate commercial water extraction, and spearhead rural off-grid solar electrification.

**Vision**: Guaranteed access to potable drinking water and reliable, clean energy for all households and industries in Plateau State.

**Mission**: To optimize water resources and renewable energy through sustainable infrastructure development, water safety monitoring, and community stewardship.

## Key Departments and Directorates
- Department of Urban Water Supply & Dam Operations
- Department of Rural Water Supply & Sanitation (PRUWASSA liaison)
- Department of Energy & Power Development
- Department of Water Quality Control & Hydrology
- Department of Planning, Research & Statistics

## Public Services and Citizen Assistance
- New residential and commercial connections to the public piped municipal water network
- Borehole drilling permits and commercial groundwater exploitation monitoring
- Water quality laboratory testing and drinking water safety certification
- Deployment of solar mini-grids and clean energy power installations for rural communities

## Supervised Parastatals and Tertiary Institutions
- Plateau State Water Board (PSWB)
- Plateau State Energy Corporation (PSEC)
- Plateau Rural Water Supply and Sanitation Agency (PRUWASSA)
