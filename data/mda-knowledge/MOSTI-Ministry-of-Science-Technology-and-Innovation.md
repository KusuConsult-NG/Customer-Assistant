# MOSTI — Ministry of Science, Technology and Innovation
Also known as: Ministry of Science and Technology

## Current Leadership
- **Commissioner**: Dr. Cletus Bako Shurkuk
- **Role Overview**: Reassigned from Ministry of Health in cabinet restructuring to drive innovation, research, and technical education.

## Past Leadership & Administrative History
- **Former Commissioner**: Hon. Danladi Mann (2019 – 2023 (Lalong administration))
- **Former Commissioner**: Hon. Musa Sule (2015 – 2019 (Lalong administration))
- **Former Commissioner**: Mr. Gyang Tanko (2007 – 2015 (Jang administration))

Promotes science culture, school science laboratories, technology adoption, and intellectual property development.

## Mandate and Core Functions
Promote scientific research and technological innovations, support STEM education in schools, foster indigenous technical inventions, facilitate intellectual property registrations, and collaborate with tech startups.

**Vision**: A knowledge-driven and technologically advanced Plateau State applying science and innovation for sustainable industrialization.

**Mission**: To incubate technological innovations, build scientific capacity among youth, and deploy practical science solutions to societal challenges.

## Key Departments and Directorates
- Department of Science Promotion & STEM Education
- Department of Technology Transfer & Incubation
- Department of Innovation, Research & Intellectual Property
- Department of Information Technology Systems
- Department of Planning, Research & Statistics

## Public Services and Citizen Assistance
- STEM school science equipment support and state science competitions sponsorship
- Patent application advisory and intellectual property assistance for indigenous inventors
- Technology incubation support and startup mentorship linkages
- Vocational and technical skills development workshops

## Current Projects, Strategic Initiatives & Ongoing Activities
- Rural Renewable Energy & Solar Mini-Grids: Installing solar power systems and mini-grids in rural primary healthcare centers, public schools, and government facilities across off-grid communities in Plateau State.
- Plateau State STEM Education Modernization: Equipping public secondary school science laboratories with modern laboratory equipment, computer workstations, and robotics learning kits.
- Plateau Innovation and Invention Grant Scheme: Providing technical grants, prototype incubation, and intellectual property patenting support for grassroots inventors and young technical innovators.
