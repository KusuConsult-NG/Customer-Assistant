### NFWP-SU: Women Affinity Groups Programme — Mangu, Mikang and Jos South
- Code: PL-2026-084 | LGA: Jos South, Mangu, Mikang | Agency: Ministry of Women Affairs and Social Development | Status: ongoing (0%)
- Details: The Nigeria for Women Project Scale-Up organises economically active women aged 18 and above into Women Affinity Groups (WAGs) for savings, financial literacy, livelihood support and capacity building. In Plateau State it is coordinated by the State Project Coordination Unit under the Ministry of Women Affairs and Social Development in Mangu (learning LGA), Mikang and Jos South, targeting 3,600 WAGs. About 1,900 potential WAGs had been identified by November 2026, and Ward Facilitators were recruited from 951 applicants. WAG formation is due November–December 2026, with expansion to Bassa, Kanam and Langtang South planned. Source: NFWP-SU Project Progress and Achievements report (Plateau SPCU, November 2026).
