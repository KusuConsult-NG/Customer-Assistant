### Installation of solar panels/ batteries/ light and upgrading of broadcast equipment
- Code: PL-2025-057 | LGA: Jos North, Jos South | Agency: Plateau Radio Television Corporation | Status: completed (0%) | Contract Sum: ₦227.00 million
- Details: Through the State Government’s intervention, solar panels, batteries and solar lighting systems were procured for PRTVC to improve power supply, reduce energy costs and ensure operational efficiency. Additionally, funds were approved for the upgrade of broadcast equipment to enhance transmission quality, expand signal coverage and extend the Corporation’s reach to a wider audience. These interventions are aimed at strengthening PRTVC’s infrastructure, improving programme content and ensuring more effective service delivery to its teeming audience across Plateau State and beyond. Delivered: Solar panels/ batteries/ lights: 30 Rayfield office; WiFi connectivity/ Laptops/ Desktops: 5 ICT unit; HD Cameras/ Tripods: 3 Newsroom/ Programmes.
