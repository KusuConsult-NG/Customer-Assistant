# PLASMEDA — Plateau State Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises Development Agency
Also known as: Plateau State Micro, Small and Medium Enterprises Development Agency (PLASMEDA), Plateau State Microfinance Development Agency

## Current Leadership
- **Director General**: Mr. Bomkam Ali Wuyep
- **Role Overview**: Leading MSME development, microcredit linkages, and entrepreneurship incubation.

## Past Leadership & Administrative History
- **Former Director General**: Mr. Haggai Haruna Gutap (2016 – 2023 (Lalong administration))

Created to catalyze micro and small businesses, enhance financial inclusion, and provide vocational incubation for youth and women.

## Mandate and Core Functions
Stimulate micro, small, and medium enterprise growth; provide financial literacy; facilitate access to low-interest credit; train entrepreneurs; and create business development hubs across Plateau State.

**Vision**: A vibrant entrepreneurial ecosystem driving wealth creation, self-employment, and industrial value addition.

**Mission**: To empower micro and small enterprise owners through capacity building, micro-credit access, business formalization, and technological incubation.

## Key Departments and Directorates
- Department of Enterprise Training & Capacity Building
- Department of Micro-Credit Linkages & Financial Inclusion
- Department of Business Development Services & Incubation
- Department of Monitoring, Evaluation & MSME Database

## Public Services and Citizen Assistance
- Free entrepreneurship, bookkeeping, and digital marketing training for small business owners
- Linkage to Bank of Industry (BOI), Development Bank of Nigeria (DBN), and microfinance loan schemes
- Assistance with corporate business registration (CAC) and NAFDAC product certifications
- Access to state-supported business incubation centres and shared tool facilities
