# SDGs-PSU — Plateau State Sustainable Development Goals Project Support Unit
Also known as: Plateau State Sustainable Development Goals, Plateau SDGs Unit

## Current Leadership
- **SDGs Project Coordinator**: Hon. Mrs. Nanbam Esther Danjuma-Lot
- **Role Overview**: Appointed by Governor Mutfwang. Leads SDG implementation across Plateau State's 17 LGAs covering human capital, poverty alleviation, climate action, and inclusive governance.

## Past Leadership & Administrative History
- **Former Project Coordinator**: Mr. Sam Damla (2015 – 2023 (Lalong administration))

Coordinates donor and state co-funded micro-projects delivering rural water, primary healthcare clinics, and school classrooms across the 17 LGAs.

## Mandate and Core Functions
Mainstream the 17 United Nations Sustainable Development Goals into state development plans, execute counterpart-funded community micro-projects, and monitor progress on poverty eradication, health, education, and clean water.

**Vision**: A prosperous Plateau State where every rural community achieves sustainable benchmarks in health, education, clean water, and food security.

**Mission**: To localize the SDGs through transparent community project execution, inter-agency synergy, and grassroots citizen participation.

## Key Departments and Directorates
- Department of Project Planning & Counterpart Operations
- Department of Community Social Mobilization
- Department of Monitoring, Evaluation & Reporting

## Public Services and Citizen Assistance
- Rural community solar borehole drilling and motorized water supply installations
- Construction and equipping of Primary Healthcare Centres and rural maternal health clinics
- Rehabilitation of basic school classroom blocks and supply of student desks in underserved villages
