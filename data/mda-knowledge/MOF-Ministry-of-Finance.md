# MOF — Ministry of Finance
Also known as: Plateau State Ministry of Finance

## Current Leadership
- **Commissioner**: Hon. Grace Dongkum Shwarta
- **Permanent Secretary**: Mrs. Ladi Agwom
- **Role Overview**: Reassigned from Secondary Education during cabinet restructuring to lead state public financial management.

## Past Leadership & Administrative History
- **Former Commissioner**: Mrs. Grace Shwarka (August 2023 – February 2025 (Mutfwang cabinet))
- **Former Commissioner**: Dr. Regina Soemlat (2019 – 2023 (Lalong administration))
- **Former Commissioner**: Hon. Tamwakat Weli (2015 – 2019 (Lalong administration))
- **Former Commissioner**: Mr. Davou Mang (2007 – 2015 (Jang administration))

Manages state fiscal discipline, sovereign loan management, and disbursements of monthly statutory allocations and internally generated revenue.

## Mandate and Core Functions
Formulate and implement state fiscal policies, maintain internal financial controls, manage state cash flow and sovereign investments, disburse approved funds, and coordinate tax policy and debt management.

**Vision**: A financially sound, resilient, and transparent government that optimizes public revenue and expenditure for economic transformation.

**Mission**: To ensure integrity, efficiency, and accountability in the generation, allocation, and accounting of Plateau State financial resources.

## Key Departments and Directorates
- Department of Consolidated Revenue Fund & Treasury
- Department of Financial Regulations & Expenditure Monitoring
- Department of Public Debt & Capital Market Operations
- Department of Internal Audit & Quality Assurance
- Department of Administration & Personnel

## Public Services and Citizen Assistance
- Contractor and vendor verification, registration, and payment status tracking
- Civil servant and pensioner payroll inquiries and electronic payment confirmation
- Fiscal transparency reporting under State Fiscal Transparency, Accountability and Sustainability (SFTAS) guidelines
- Inter-governmental fiscal transfers and local government joint account monitoring

## Supervised Parastatals and Tertiary Institutions
- Office of the Accountant General (OAG)
- Plateau State Internal Revenue Service (PSIRS)
- Plateau State Debt Management Department (DMO)
