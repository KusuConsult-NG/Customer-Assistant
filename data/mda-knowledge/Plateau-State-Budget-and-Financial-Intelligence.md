# Plateau State Budget and Financial Intelligence

Also known as: Plateau State Budget, State Appropriation, Plateau Finances, MoBEP Budget Intelligence
Alternative names: Plateau State Annual Budget, Citizens' Budget, Plateau State Appropriation Law, Plateau Revenue and Expenditure

## Overview and Legal Framework
The Plateau State Government budget is coordinated, prepared, and monitored by the **Ministry of Budget and Economic Planning (MoBEP)** under the leadership of the Honorable Commissioner, Hon. Chrysanthus Dawam, and assented into law by the Executive Governor of Plateau State, Barr. Caleb Manasseh Mutfwang.

Every figure presented represents official appropriations enacted by the Plateau State House of Assembly. In accordance with state financial guidelines:
1. **Approved Allocation vs. Actual Expenditure**: An approved allocation represents an authorized spending ceiling. It does not establish that funds have been fully disbursed or that a physical project has been completed.
2. **Capital vs. Recurrent**: Capital expenditures fund physical infrastructure, public assets, and equipment. Recurrent expenditures fund civil service salaries, pensions, gratuities, and operational overheads.
3. **Sources**: Figures are derived from the Plateau State Appropriation Laws, official State Executive Council gazettes, Citizens' Budget publications, and published implementation reports.

## 2025 Approved State Budget ("Budget of Sustainability and Economic Transformation")

- **Total Budget Size**: **₦468,485,391,120.00** (₦468.49 billion)
- **Year-on-Year Growth**: +48.79% increase over the 2024 approved budget of ₦314.86 billion
- **Legal Status**: Passed by the Plateau State House of Assembly and signed into law by Governor Caleb M. Mutfwang

### Expenditure Breakdown (2025)
- **Capital Expenditure**: **₦285,301,186,710.00** (60.9% of total budget)
  - Focused on statewide road networks, healthcare facility upgrades, agricultural industrialization, digital economy broadband expansion, and school modernization.
- **Recurrent Expenditure**: **₦183,184,204,410.00** (39.1% of total budget)
  - **Personnel Costs**: ₦108.50 billion (Civil servant salaries, implementation of the new national minimum wage, teacher recruitments, pensions, and gratuities).
  - **Overhead Costs**: ₦74.68 billion (Running costs, administrative logistics, utility maintenance, and inter-agency operations).

### Revenue Sources (2025 Projections)
- **Internally Generated Revenue (IGR)**: **₦62,540,000,000.00** (₦62.54 billion), mobilized through the Plateau State Internal Revenue Service (PSIRS) via digital land titling, automated tax filing, and commercial formalization.
- **Statutory Allocation & VAT**: **₦238,120,000,000.00** (₦238.12 billion) from the Federation Account Allocation Committee (FAAC) and Value Added Tax receipts.
- **Capital Receipts, Grants & Concessional Financing**: **₦167,825,391,120.00** (₦167.83 billion) from multilateral development partners (World Bank, AfDB), federal counterpart funding, and sovereign green bonds.

### Sectoral Allocations (2025)
1. **Works, Infrastructure, Roads & Transport**:
   - **Allocation**: **₦92,450,000,000.00** (₦92.45 billion | 19.73% of budget)
   - **Lead Agencies**: Ministry of Works, Plateau State Road Maintenance Agency (PLARAMA), Ministry of Transport, Plateau Express Services Group Ltd (PESGL).
   - **Priority Projects**: Inter-LGA arterial highway rehabilitation, dualization of metropolitan corridors, Tin City Metro bus expansion across southern and central zones.
2. **Education and Human Capital Development**:
   - **Allocation**: **₦68,920,000,000.00** (₦68.92 billion | 14.71% of budget)
   - **Lead Agencies**: Ministry of Education, Plateau State Universal Basic Education Board (PSUBEB), Plateau State Polytechnic, Plateau State University (PLASU) Bokkos, College of Education Gindiri.
   - **Priority Projects**: Smart classroom deployments, primary school rehabilitation, vocational and STEM training facilities, teacher recruitment and accreditation.
3. **Healthcare, Medical Services and Social Insurance**:
   - **Allocation**: **₦44,650,000,000.00** (₦44.65 billion | 9.53% of budget)
   - **Lead Agencies**: Ministry of Health, PLASCHEMA (Contributory Healthcare), PSPHCB (Primary Health Care Board), Plateau Specialist Hospital (PSSH), PSDMCMA (Drugs and Medical Commodities).
   - **Priority Projects**: Universal Health Coverage expansion through PLASCHEMA vulnerable group funds, stocking of the new central medical store, upgrade of General Hospitals in Barkin Ladi, Mangu, and Shendam.
4. **Agriculture, Food Security and Agro-Processing**:
   - **Allocation**: **₦39,200,000,000.00** (₦39.20 billion | 8.37% of budget)
   - **Lead Agencies**: Ministry of Agriculture and Natural Resources, Plateau Agricultural Development Programme (PADP), Ministry of Livestock Development.
   - **Priority Projects**: Subsidized fertilizer distribution, modern cold-chain potato storage facilities, tractor mechanization hiring units, dry-season irrigation dams.
5. **Land Administration, Housing and Urban Development**:
   - **Allocation**: **₦24,800,000,000.00** (₦24.80 billion | 5.29% of budget)
   - **Lead Agencies**: Ministry of Lands, Survey and Town Planning (MLSTP), Jos Metropolitan Development Board (JMDB), Ministry of Housing and Urban Development.
   - **Priority Projects**: PLAGIS digital land registry completion, Jos master plan modernization, worker housing estates.
6. **Water Resources, Sanitation and Energy**:
   - **Allocation**: **₦21,300,000,000.00** (₦21.30 billion | 4.55% of budget)
   - **Lead Agencies**: Ministry of Water Resources and Energy Development, Plateau State Water Board (PSWB), Plateau State Energy Corporation (PSEC), PEPSA.
   - **Priority Projects**: Plateau rural water supply schemes, Jos-Bukuru water distribution rehabilitation, mini-grid solar electrification.
7. **Science, Technology, Innovation & Digital Economy**:
   - **Allocation**: **₦11,540,000,000.00** (₦11.54 billion | 2.46% of budget)
   - **Lead Agencies**: Plateau Information Communication and Technology Development Agency (PICTDA), Ministry of Science, Technology and Innovation.
   - **Priority Projects**: 2,500km statewide fiber optic broadband backbone with Solutron Eastern Fibre, LGA Innovation and CBT Hubs, Code Plateau 6.0 fellowship, e-governance civil service automation.
8. **Tourism, Culture and Hospitality**:
   - **Allocation**: **₦8,200,000,000.00** (₦8.20 billion | 1.75% of budget)
   - **Lead Agencies**: Ministry of Tourism, Culture and Hospitality, Plateau State Tourism Corporation (PSTC).
   - **Priority Projects**: Infrastructure upgrade of Pandam Game Reserve, Kurra Falls, Assop Falls, and Jos Wildlife Park.

## Frequently Asked Financial Questions

### How can a citizen verify a state budget figure?
Citizens can verify budget figures through official publications by the Ministry of Budget and Economic Planning (MoBEP) on plateaustate.gov.ng or visit the Ministry at the J.D. Gomwalk State Secretariat Complex, Jos.

### Does an approved budget mean the money was already spent?
No. An approved budget represents statutory authorization by the House of Assembly allowing MDAs to incur expenditure. Actual expenditure depends on cash availability, revenue realization, and warrant releases issued by the Ministry of Finance and Accountant General's Office.
