# MYSD — Ministry of Youth and Sports Development
Also known as: Ministry of Youth Development, Ministry of Youth and Sports, Ministry of Youth and Sports Development (MYSD)

## Current Leadership
- **Commissioner for Youth and Sports Development**: Hon. Bashir Lawandi Datti
- **Role Overview**: Returned to cabinet June 2026 after earlier resignation to contest primaries. Previously served as Commissioner for Water Resources and Energy Development.

## Past Leadership & Administrative History
- **Former Commissioner**: Mr. Bashir Lawandi Datti (August 2023 – February 2025 (reassigned to Water Resources))
- **Former Commissioner**: Hon. Victor Lapang (2018 – 2023 (Lalong administration))
- **Former Commissioner**: Alh. Dayyabu Garga (2015 – 2018 (Lalong administration))
- **Former Commissioner**: Hon. James Pam (2007 – 2015 (Jang administration))

Responsible for mobilizing youth potential, vocational entrepreneurship, state athletic teams, and sports stadium facilities.

## Mandate and Core Functions
Formulate youth empowerment policies, organize vocational training for unemployed youth, administer state sports competitions, maintain stadia, and supervise state-owned sports teams (Plateau United Football Club and Mighty Jets FC).

**Vision**: Empowered, self-reliant, and socially responsible Plateau youth, backed by sporting excellence in national and international arenas.

**Mission**: To unlock youth talents through structured entrepreneurship schemes, leadership mentorship, and grassroots athletic programmes.

## Key Departments and Directorates
- Department of Youth Mobilization & Development
- Department of Sports Administration & Competitions
- Department of Youth Vocational Skills & Entrepreneurship
- Department of Facilities & Stadia Management
- Department of Planning, Research & Statistics

## Public Services and Citizen Assistance
- Registration, screening, and certification of youth clubs, associations, and NGOs
- Enrollment in state youth vocational skills acquisition and digital training bootcamps
- Grassroots sporting competitions talent scouting and athletics mentoring
- Stadium usage permits for public and private sporting events (New Jos Stadium Zaria Road, Rwang Pam Stadium)

## Supervised Parastatals and Tertiary Institutions
- Plateau State Sports Council
- Plateau United Football Club
- Mighty Jets International FC
