# MARD — Ministry of Agriculture and Natural Resources
Also known as: Ministry of Agriculture, Ministry of Agriculture and Rural Development

## Current Leadership
- **Commissioner**: Hon. Samson Ishaku Bugama
- **Permanent Secretary**: Dr. Jonathan B. Danladi
- **Role Overview**: Supervises state agrarian transformation, food security, and farmer empowerment.

## Past Leadership & Administrative History
- **Former Commissioner**: Dr. Hosea Finangwai (2019 – 2023 (Lalong administration))
- **Former Commissioner**: Mrs. Linda Barau (2015 – 2019 (Lalong administration))
- **Former Commissioner**: Hon. Steven Barko (2011 – 2015 (Jang administration))

Formerly oversaw veterinary services and livestock until the creation of the dedicated Ministry of Livestock Development, Veterinary Services and Fisheries in February 2025. Coordinates closely with the Federal Ministry of Agriculture and Food Security.

## Mandate and Core Functions
Formulate, execute, and monitor state agricultural policies, spearhead food security programs, distribute subsidized agricultural inputs (fertilizers, chemicals, improved seedlings), maintain farm mechanization tractor hiring schemes, and promote agricultural extension services to smallholder farmers.

**Vision**: A modern, productive, and competitive agricultural sector ensuring sustainable food security, poverty reduction, and economic wealth for Plateau State.

**Mission**: To empower farmers through cutting-edge extension services, modern mechanization, high-yield inputs, and agro-allied value-chain development across all 17 LGAs.

## Public Services and Citizen Assistance
- Subsidized inorganic and organic fertilizer distribution through designated ward collection centres
- Tractor hiring and farm implements leasing for cooperative farming groups
- Certified high-yield disease-resistant crop seedlings distribution (maize, Irish potato, soya beans, rice)
- Agricultural extension advisory visits and pest outbreak interventions
- Dry-season fadama irrigation support and small water pumps allocation
- Registration and certification of farmer cooperative societies

## Current Projects, Strategic Initiatives & Ongoing Activities
- Direct Fertilizer Subsidy Distribution: Direct ward-level distribution of subsidized inorganic and organic fertilizers, certified crop seeds, and crop protection chemicals to smallholder farmers.
- Irish Potato Value Chain & Blight Control Project: Tissue culture seed multiplication and modern cold storage facilities in Mangu, Bokkos, and Barkin Ladi to combat potato late blight disease.
- Agricultural Mechanization & Tractor Leasing Scheme: Revitalizing tractor hiring units across the three senatorial districts to enhance mechanized agriculture.

## Supervised Parastatals and Tertiary Institutions
