# OHCS — Office of the Head of Civil Service
Also known as: Head of Service

## Current Leadership
- **Head of Civil Service**: Mr. Stephen Pam Gadong
- **Role Overview**: Appointed acting August 2024 and confirmed substantive Head of Civil Service December 2024, succeeding Barr. Rauta Dakok who retired at the mandatory age of 60.

## Past Leadership & Administrative History
- **Former Head of Civil Service**: Engr. Sunday Hyat (2021 – 2023 (Lalong administration))
- **Former Head of Civil Service**: Mr. Izam Azi (2015 – 2021 (Lalong administration))

Responsible for public service personnel management, administrative leadership, and civil service reforms.

## Mandate and Core Functions
Manage the human capital of the Plateau State civil service, foster meritocracy and discipline, organize staff development and civil service examinations, administer pensions, and maintain harmonious labour relations.

**Vision**: A professional, ethical, and high-performing civil service dedicated to excellence in public governance.

**Mission**: To build a competent, well-motivated workforce utilizing modern technologies to deliver public services efficiently to Plateau citizens.

## Key Departments and Directorates
- Establishment & Service Matters
- Staff Development & Training
- Public Service Reforms
- Pensions & Gratuities Administration
- Labour Relations & Staff Welfare

## Public Services and Citizen Assistance
- Civil service career appointments, promotions, gazetting, and service records verification
- Retired public servants pension enrollment, biometrics, and gratuity processing
- Civil service internship, NYSC placement, and industrial attachment postings
- Public service grievance mediation and labour dispute resolutions
