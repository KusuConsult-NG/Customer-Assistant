# LGSC — Plateau State Local Government Service Commission
Also known as: Plateau State Local Government Service Commission (LGSC), LGSC

## Current Leadership
- **Chairman, Local Government Service Commission**: Chief Simon Tangni
- **Role Overview**: Leading unified local government personnel administration across all 17 LGAs.

## Past Leadership & Administrative History
- **Former Chairman**: Hon. Robert Taple (2015 – 2023 (Lalong administration))

The central personnel authority for the unified local government service across the 17 Local Government Areas of Plateau State.

## Mandate and Core Functions
Recruit, appoint, post, transfer, promote, and discipline senior local government administrative and technical staff across all 17 LGAs; conduct service examinations; and maintain unified personnel standards.

**Vision**: A dedicated, professional, and efficient local government workforce driving grassroots governance and community prosperity.

**Mission**: To ensure fairness, merit, and high ethical conduct in the management of local government employees across Plateau State.

## Key Departments and Directorates
- Department of Appointments, Promotions & Transfers
- Department of Discipline & Appeals
- Department of Training & Human Resource Development
- Department of Local Government Pension Administration

## Public Services and Citizen Assistance
- Recruitment and confirmation of unified local government career officers across the 17 LGAs
- Inter-local government transfers and career promotion board assessments
- Disciplinary appeal hearings for aggrieved local government staff
- Local government staff pension documentation and gratuity computations
