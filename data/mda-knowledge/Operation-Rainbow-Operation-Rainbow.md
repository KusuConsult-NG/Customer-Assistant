# Operation Rainbow — Operation Rainbow
Also known as: Operation Rainbow Plateau State, Plateau State Neighborhood Watch

## Current Leadership
- **State Coordinator**: Brig.-Gen. G.G. Shipi (rtd)
- **Role Overview**: Leading community neighborhood watch, early warning intelligence, and civil protection.

## Past Leadership & Administrative History
- **Former Coordinator**: Maj.-Gen. Nicholas Rogers (rtd) (2019 – 2023)

Created under Governor Jonah Jang and strengthened under successive administrations as a state-backed community security intelligence outfit.

## Mandate and Core Functions
Maintain neighborhood vigilance, collect grassroots early-warning security intelligence, protect rural communities from armed attacks, foster inter-ethnic peace, and liaise closely with federal security forces (Police, Operation Safe Haven, Civil Defence).

**Vision**: A safe, peaceful, and secure Plateau State where communities live without fear of armed violence.

**Mission**: To provide proactive community surveillance, early warning alert networks, and peaceful civil defense support to safeguard Plateau lives and farmlands.

## Key Departments and Directorates
- Directorate of Community Neighborhood Operations
- Directorate of Intelligence & Early Warning Gathering
- Directorate of Training & Civil Protection
- Directorate of Inter-Agency Liaison

## Public Services and Citizen Assistance
- 24-hour toll-free reporting of imminent security threats, suspicious armed movements, and distress calls in rural communities
- Community neighborhood patrol coordination and early warning de-escalation
- Protection of farming communities and conflict hotspot liaison
