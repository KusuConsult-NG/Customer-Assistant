# OSGS — Office of the Secretary to the Government of the State
Also known as: Office of the Secretary to the State Government, SSG Office

## Current Leadership
- **Secretary to the Government of the State**: Arc. Samuel Nanchang Jatau
- **Role Overview**: Coordinates all state ministries, boards, commissions, and implementation of executive council decisions.

## Past Leadership & Administrative History
- **Former Secretary to the State Government**: Prof. Danladi Abok Atu (2019 – 2023 (Lalong administration))
- **Former Secretary to the State Government**: Hon. Rufus Bature (2015 – 2018 (Lalong administration))
- **Former Secretary to the State Government**: Prof. Shedrack Best (2011 – 2015 (Jang administration))

The central clearinghouse of the Plateau State Government, providing policy continuity, cabinet secretariat operations, and state protocol management.

## Mandate and Core Functions
Coordinate government policies across all MDAs, serve as Secretariat to the State Executive Council, supervise implementation of council decisions, manage national honours and protocol, and coordinate inter-governmental relations.

**Vision**: An agile, coordinated, and accountable civil governance apparatus implementing the Governor's transformation agenda.

**Mission**: To ensure seamless synergy among all arms and agencies of government, foster policy coherence, and maintain security and inter-faith harmony.

## Key Departments and Directorates
- Cabinet Secretariat
- Special Services Directorate (Security & Intelligence Liaison)
- General Administration Directorate
- Political & Inter-Governmental Affairs
- State Protocol Directorate

## Public Services and Citizen Assistance
- Receiving, processing, and channeling public petitions to appropriate ministries and commissions
- Coordinating official state ceremonies, national day celebrations, and inter-denominational events
- Liaising with federal agencies, diplomatic corps, and international missions visiting Plateau State
