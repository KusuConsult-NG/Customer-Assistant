# CAST — College of Arts, Science and Technology, Kurgwi
Also known as: College of Arts, Science and Technology, Kurgwi (CAST), CAST Kurgwi

## Current Leadership
- **Provost**: Mr. Birmiap Patrick Yilpoe
- **Role Overview**: Leading academic curriculum accreditation, technical diploma programs, and IJMB tertiary preparation.

## Past Leadership & Administrative History
- **Former Provost**: Dr. Stephen Hirse (Pre-2023)

Plateau State tertiary academic institution situated in Southern Senatorial District, providing diploma and IJMB tertiary pre-degree education.

## Mandate and Core Functions
Provide qualitative tertiary education, Interim Joint Matriculation Board (IJMB) pre-degree courses, National Diploma (ND) programs in arts, sciences, and management, and technical vocational certificates.

**Vision**: A reputable tertiary polytechnic-style institution renowned for academic discipline, practical science, and technological innovation.

**Mission**: To prepare students for direct university entry and gainful technical employment through rigorous academic and technical training.

## Public Services and Citizen Assistance
- Admission applications for IJMB, Remedial, and National Diploma programmes
- Academic transcript processing, certificate issuance, and student verification
- Community adult literacy and vocational computer training classes
