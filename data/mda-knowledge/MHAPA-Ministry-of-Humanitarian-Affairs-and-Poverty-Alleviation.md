# MHAPA — Ministry of Humanitarian Affairs and Poverty Alleviation
Also known as: Ministry of Humanitarian Affairs, Ministry of Humanitarian Affairs, Disaster Management and Social Development

## Current Leadership
- **Commissioner**: Hon. Ishaku Maren / Prince Miaphen
- **Role Overview**: Newly created ministry established in the 2025/2026 restructuring to address displacement, emergency relief, and social safety nets.

## Past Leadership & Administrative History
- **Former Supervisory Coordinator**: Hon. Caroline Panglang Dafur (2023 – 2025 (as Women Affairs & Social Welfare))

Created by Governor Caleb Mutfwang to provide focused executive attention on internally displaced persons (IDPs), disaster rehabilitation, and targeted social welfare interventions following security challenges.

## Mandate and Core Functions
Coordinate emergency disaster response, provide relief materials to victims of floods, storms, and civil unrest; manage IDP camps and resettlement; implement conditional cash transfers; and drive poverty eradication schemes.

**Vision**: A compassionate, resilient Plateau State where vulnerable citizens, displaced families, and crisis victims are rapidly supported and permanently rehabilitated.

**Mission**: To deliver prompt, dignified, and accountable humanitarian assistance, disaster relief, and sustainable poverty alleviation interventions.

## Key Departments and Directorates
- Department of Emergency Relief & Disaster Management
- Department of IDP Rehabilitation & Resettlement
- Department of Social Safety Nets & Conditional Cash Transfers
- Department of Poverty Alleviation & Livelihood Empowerment
- Department of Donor & Humanitarian Agency Coordination

## Public Services and Citizen Assistance
- Emergency relief food and non-food supplies distribution to disaster-affected communities
- Registration, profiling, and psycho-social support for Internally Displaced Persons (IDPs)
- Disaster casualty and property damage assessment for government compensations/grants
- Coordination of state social register and cash transfer enrollments for poorest households

## Supervised Parastatals and Tertiary Institutions
- Plateau State Emergency Management Agency (PLASEMA / SEMA)
