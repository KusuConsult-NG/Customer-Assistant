# PICTDA — Plateau Information Communication and Technology Development Agency
Also known as: Plateau Information Communication and Technology Development Agency (PICTDA), Plateau State Information and Communication Technology Development Agency, PICTDA

## Current Leadership
- **Director General**: Mr. Datong Dominic Gwaman
- **Role Overview**: Leading Plateau State digital transformation, government cloud infrastructure, and tech innovation ecosystem.

## Past Leadership & Administrative History
- **Former Director General**: Mr. Daser David (2018 – 2023 (Founding Director General))

Established in 2018 to pioneer state digital governance, manage government cloud services, build the Code Plateau tech workforce, and establish IT standards.

## Mandate and Core Functions
Formulate and execute state ICT policy; deploy and manage government email, web portals, and connectivity networks; administer the Code Plateau software engineering training academy; protect government cyber infrastructure; and catalyze the digital economy.

**Vision**: To position Plateau State as Nigeria's leading technological innovation ecosystem and preferred digital outsourcing destination.

**Mission**: To leverage digital technology for open governance, build a globally competitive technical workforce, and automate government services for citizen convenience.

## Key Departments and Directorates
- Department of E-Governance & Software Systems
- Department of Infrastructure, Networks & Cybersecurity
- Department of Digital Skills & Workforce Development (Code Plateau)
- Department of IT Standards, Policy & Regulatory Compliance
- Department of Innovation, Research & Startup Incubation

## Public Services and Citizen Assistance
- Code Plateau Fellowship: Intensive training in full-stack software development, UI/UX design, data science, and cloud computing for youth
- Government official email and subdomain provisioning for MDAs (plateaustate.gov.ng)
- Tech startup incubation, co-working facilities, and digital investor linkages
- Open Government Data portal maintenance and ICT equipment standards testing

## Current Projects, Strategic Initiatives & Ongoing Activities
- State-Wide Broadband Fibre Infrastructure Expansion: Expanding high-speed fibre-optic coverage from 900 km to 2,500 km across Plateau State in a public-private partnership with Solutron Eastern Fibre Limited, providing a high-speed digital backbone for e-governance, telemedicine, smart security, and traffic management.
- Code Plateau Fellowship: Flagship human capital development program operating from the Code Plateau Campus in Jos, providing intensive, tuition-free training in full-stack software development, UI/UX design, data science, and digital marketing. Includes 'Code Plateau on Wheels' for rural LGA digital outreach and 'Code for Plateau' talent placements within state MDAs.
- Federal 3 Million Technical Talents (3MTT) Partnership: Partnering with the Federal Ministry of Communications, Innovation & Digital Economy to train thousands of Plateau residents in cybersecurity, AI, cloud computing, data science, and software engineering with prioritized female participation.
- LGA Computer-Based Test (CBT) & Grassroots Innovation Centres: Establishing modern digital examination and innovation hubs across Plateau State's 17 Local Government Areas (including the Qua'an Pan Innovation Hub, Murtala Mohammed House 6th Floor Tech Hub, and regional training centers).
- Government E-Governance & Data-Driven Digital Migration: Digitizing school management systems, automating civil service payroll and biometric verification, digitalizing judiciary case files, and provisioning secure official email addresses and subdomains on plateaustate.gov.ng for all MDAs.
- ICT Procurement Technical Clearance & Vendor Registry: Enforcing mandatory technical review and compliance clearance for all government hardware and software procurement across state MDAs, ensuring IT standards, cost efficiency, and priority patronage for local tech enterprises.
- Silicon Plateau & Tech Ecosystem Collaboration: Organizing the Plateau State Tech Summit and partnering with NITDA, World Bank, Plateau Association of Tech Companies (PLATEC), Nigeria Computer Society (NCS), and private tech hubs (nHub) to attract global venture funding and tech outsourcing to Jos.
